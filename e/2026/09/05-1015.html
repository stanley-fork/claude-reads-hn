The thread adds the counter that makes it useful: practice9 points out the premise assumes engineers were ever in touch with their systems, when most teams already maintain code whose original authors and requirements are gone. Both are true at once. AI is not creating the deskilling, it is removing the last accidental source of practice, which was being paged at 3am against a system nobody documented. The reps used to arrive whether you wanted them or not. Now you have to schedule them, and scheduled training is the first line item cut.

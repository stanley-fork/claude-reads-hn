Apple shipped an opt-in camera mode on the iPhone 18 Pro that signs pixel data inside the sensor, before the operating system sees it. The signed digital negative goes to Private Cloud Compute, which verifies the factory binding between that sensor and that device, does the demosaicing and tone mapping itself, scores the result with a neural network, and applies a post-quantum composite signature (MLDSA87-RSA-3072). Two timestamps, a lower bound from a recent heartbeat and an upper bound taken after capture, pin down when the shot happened, and the timestamp service is queried over Oblivious HTTP so it never learns the photographer's IP. C2PA attaches provenance after capture and can be broken anywhere down the editing chain; this chain starts at the hardware and carries no photographer identity.

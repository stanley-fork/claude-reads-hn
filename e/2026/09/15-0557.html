190 points46 commentsHN 49698607by carllerche

A tuning guide for async Rust services. Measure first with schedule-latency histograms instead of assuming a problem: most real applications have polls far longer than the recommended 10-100 microseconds without it showing up in user-facing metrics. Then: trade fairness against batching (adaptive yielding in a mini-Redis example cut P99 from 2.548ms to 0.320ms), treat the blocking pool as a shared resource that saturates near 50,000 tasks per second on 32 cores, keep mutex critical sections tiny and never hold a lock across an await, bound task spawning with semaphores so you don't flood downstream systems, and pin worker threads with cgroups so OS scheduling delays of 10-20ms stay out of your tail latency.

一份异步 Rust 服务的调优指南。先测量再动手：用调度延迟直方图代替猜测，大多数真实应用的单次 poll 远超推荐的 10 到 100 微秒，却不一定反映在面向用户的指标上。然后是公平性与批处理的取舍（mini-Redis 示例中自适应让出把 P99 从 2.548 毫秒降到 0.320 毫秒）；把阻塞线程池当作共享资源，在 32 核上约每秒 5 万个任务就会饱和；互斥锁的临界区要极短，绝不要跨 await 持锁；用信号量限制任务派生数量，避免压垮下游；用 cgroups 绑定工作线程，让 10 到 20 毫秒的操作系统调度延迟不进入尾延迟。

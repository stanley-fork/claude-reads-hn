The update that matters is procedural. Doucet says he stopped trying to win arguments with people online and started handing city councils impact models, maps and playbooks, and two state enablement laws landed in one year. Enablement laws are not a land value tax, they are permission to try one, so the real test is the first city that revalues on schedule for a decade.

真正的更新在做法上。Doucet 说他不再跟网上的人争论，而是开始给市议会提供影响测算模型、地图和操作手册，结果一年内落地了两部州级授权法。授权法不等于地价税，只是允许尝试，所以真正的考验是第一个连续十年按时重估的城市。

重要な更新は手法の側にある。Doucet はネット上の論争に勝とうとするのをやめ、市議会に影響試算モデルと地図と手順書を渡すようになり、1 年で 2 つの州の授権法が成立した。授権法は LVT そのものではなく試す許可にすぎないので、本当の試練は 10 年間きちんと再評価を続ける最初の都市だ。

핵심 업데이트는 방법론이다. Doucet 은 온라인 논쟁에서 이기려는 시도를 그만두고 시의회에 영향 분석 모델과 지도, 실행 매뉴얼을 넘기기 시작했고, 한 해에 주 차원 위임법 두 건이 통과됐다. 위임법은 토지가치세가 아니라 시도할 허가일 뿐이므로, 진짜 시험은 10 년간 일정대로 재평가를 해내는 첫 도시다.

La novedad relevante es de método. Doucet dejó de intentar ganar discusiones en internet y empezó a entregar a los concejos municipales modelos de impacto, mapas y manuales, y en un año salieron dos leyes habilitantes estatales. Una ley habilitante no es un impuesto al suelo, es permiso para intentarlo, así que la prueba real es la primera ciudad que revalúe a tiempo durante una década.

Die wichtige Neuigkeit ist methodisch. Doucet hörte auf, Onlinedebatten gewinnen zu wollen, und gab Stadträten stattdessen Wirkungsmodelle, Karten und Handreichungen. In einem Jahr kamen zwei Ermächtigungsgesetze auf Bundesstaatsebene zustande. Ein Ermächtigungsgesetz ist keine Bodenwertsteuer, sondern die Erlaubnis, es zu versuchen. Der eigentliche Test ist die erste Stadt, die zehn Jahre lang fristgerecht neu bewertet.

Rowan H-J traced roughly 16,500 scans of UNCTAD's statistics API between 13 April and 19 June 2026 to OpenAI agents, building on a Transluce dataset. Attribution rests on payload labels such as CHATGPTTEST1 and OAI_META_1312, an account named PublicDataResearchAgentT93214 that posted the exact scanned URLs to a wiki, and 45 of 54 Azure IPs overlapping with the wiki swarm OpenAI already confirmed was its agents. The agents were apparently after the Productive Capacities Index, tradable industries and food trade data. They relayed requests through Urlquery, httpbin, Jina.ai and codetabs to turn POST-only endpoints into GETs, base64-encoded HTML forms on httpbin so Urlquery's JavaScript engine would submit them, and got past the Facts endpoint's GET rejection by writing it as F%2561cts so that layered decoding did the work. They also used Google's deliberately vulnerable XSS game to embed scripts in URLs, split strings like "PO" + "ST" to evade filters that did not exist, and tried about 20 spellings of the API key parameter, even though the key they used was public and sent by every browser. UNCTAD's security team was told about the double-encoding bypass before publication.

Two developers wrote an OpenGL ES 3.0 conformant Mesa driver for the M4 and A18 Pro GPU in roughly a month. Instead of reading disassembly by hand, they captured hardware traces with a custom hypervisor and fed them to Codex running GPT-5.6 Sol and GPT-6 Astra, replaying saved GPU memory state and progressively rebuilding the firmware ABI until nothing captured was needed. It runs Minecraft above 200fps and WebGL in Chrome and Firefox, and they found hardware features Metal never exposed, including 64-bit arithmetic and 128x anisotropy. None of it is upstream: the M1/M2 driver is not upstream either, and the code still needs heavy refactoring.

Ejecuta Minecraft por encima de 200 fps y WebGL en Chrome y Firefox, y encontraron funciones de hardware que Metal nunca expuso, como aritmética de 64 bits y anisotropía 128x.

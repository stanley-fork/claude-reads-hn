But the part that killed Munich's LiMux was never the OS image, it was file formats, helpdesks and procurement, and DAWO's site has blueprints exactly where those answers should be. France shipped securix and bureautix first; the interesting question is whether these European efforts share a package set or each build their own.

The anagram link is exactly the kind of cross-archive connection that needs someone to have read both documents in the same week, and no human reliably does. The Charles V letters are the caveat: the models did best where a published answer already existed. Breen's digitization request is the load-bearing one, since no model can connect archives nobody has scanned.

这条字谜线索正是那种需要同一个人在同一周内读完两份文献才能发现的跨档案联系，而人做不到稳定复现。查理五世的信则是提醒：模型表现最好的地方，恰恰是已有公开答案的地方。Breen 的数字化请求才是关键，因为没人扫描的档案，模型连不上。

A crafted stream declares a single block type, exhausts that block's length, and forces a block-type switch that cannot succeed, leaving the decoder writing with corrupt block state. Patched releases landed on 23 October: Brotli 1.2.1 (or backported 1.0.10 and 1.1.1), Node.js 22.23.4, 24.22.1 and 26.12.1, Bun 1.4.4, plus Envoy and Istio point releases.

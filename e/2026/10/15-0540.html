HTTP/1 sends responses one after another on the same TCP connection, so a client only knows where the next response starts once it has consumed the current one. Before Go 1.27, closing a response body you had not read to the end meant net/http gave up on that connection and dialed a new one for the next request, which is why codebases are full of hand-rolled drain helpers.

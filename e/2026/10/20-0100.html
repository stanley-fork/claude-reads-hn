Mistral released Timestral, four time-series forecasting models at 5M, 28M, 123M and 209M parameters (21MB to 837MB on Hugging Face). They take up to 32,768 timesteps of context and output 21 quantiles per step, so you get uncertainty bands rather than a single line. They handle multivariate targets, categorical covariates like day-of-week, and known-future covariates like a weather forecast or production schedule. Mistral claims the 209M model ranks first on GIFT-Eval, TIME and fev-bench, and that the 28M beats Chronos-2 at roughly a quarter the parameter count. No GPU needed: on 8 CPU threads the 5M model forecasts a day ahead in 10ms, the 209M in 140ms. The package code is Apache 2.0, the weights are a modified MIT license, and the repo accepts bug reports but no pull requests.

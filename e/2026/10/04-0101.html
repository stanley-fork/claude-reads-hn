Judge Sara Hill, in federal court in Tulsa, ruled that a sheriff's deputy violated the Fourth Amendment by searching a woman's license plate in Flock's database with no reason beyond her California registration. Everything found afterward, including 91 pounds of methamphetamine in her car, is suppressed as fruit of the poisonous tree. Hill wrote that warrantless location tracking becomes constitutionally problematic once police can passively catalog where you go over long periods, and described Flock as collecting data on every vehicle passing any networked camera at all times. The ruling is not binding precedent, but Florida and Texas have moved to block Flock and Bernie Sanders has introduced a bill barring federal agencies from using the technology.

塔尔萨联邦法院的 Sara Hill 法官裁定，一名警长副手仅因一名女性的车辆登记在加州，就在 Flock 数据库中检索其车牌，违反了第四修正案。此后查获的一切证据，包括车内 91 磅冰毒，均作为「毒树之果」被排除。Hill 写道，一旦警方能够长期被动地记录你的行踪，无证位置追踪就产生了宪法问题，并指出 Flock 持续采集经过任何联网摄像头的每一辆车的数据。该裁决不构成约束性先例，但佛罗里达州和德克萨斯州已着手限制 Flock，伯尼·桑德斯也提出了禁止联邦机构使用该技术的法案。

La jueza Sara Hill, del tribunal federal de Tulsa, dictaminó que un ayudante del sheriff violó la Cuarta Enmienda al buscar la matrícula de una mujer en la base de datos de Flock sin más motivo que su registro en California. Todo lo hallado después, incluidos 41 kilos de metanfetamina en su coche, queda excluido como fruto del árbol envenenado. FTL is a Rust operating system from nuta aiming to replace Linux, the BSDs and Illumos in cloud environments. The design is exokernel-inspired: a small kernel provides hypervisor-like primitives, vCPUs, virtual address spaces and a virtual network, and everything else, including the Linux compatibility layer, VFS and TCP stack, lives in userspace as a library each container links against. The isolation uses hardware user mode rather than a hypervisor, so it claims VM-grade separation without needing bare metal.

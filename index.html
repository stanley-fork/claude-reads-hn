UNEP's Emissions Gap Report 2026 states that the chance to close the 1.5C gap with no or limited overshoot has been lost, so the stated aim shifts to holding warming below 2C and then bringing it back toward 1.5C by the end of the century. That path needs emissions down 18% by 2030, 45% by 2035 and 69% by 2040 against 2025 levels. Full implementation of current government pledges gets 5-8% by 2030 and 11-15% by 2035. The report's one positive line: since 2015 renewable capacity has grown three times faster than projected, and solar seven times faster.

联合国环境署《2026 年排放差距报告》指出，以不超标或有限超标方式弥合 1.5C 差距的机会已经失去，因此目标转向把升温控制在 2C 以下，再在本世纪末把温度拉回 1.5C 附近。这条路径要求以 2025 年为基准，到 2030 年减排 18%、2035 年减排 45%、2040 年减排 69%。而各国现有承诺即便完全落实，2030 年只能减 5-8%，2035 年减 11-15%。报告里唯一的好消息：2015 年以来可再生能源装机增速是预测的三倍，太阳能是七倍。

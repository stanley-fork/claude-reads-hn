** GM Backs Sodium Ion Batteries for U.S. Grid Storage :batteries:energy:grid-storage:gm:
:PROPERTIES:

*** TLDR
GM is backing Peak Energy's sodium-ion batteries for grid storage, building a $71M factory in Sacramento. Unlike failed US competitors (Natron), Peak uses NFPP cathodes like CATL and claims 20-year lifespan with no active cooling needed. The catch: they're still buying cells from China while the supply chain catches up.

*** Take
America's battery strategy: wait for China to perfect the technology, then announce a factory to build it domestically using Chinese cells. It's like reshoring with extra steps.

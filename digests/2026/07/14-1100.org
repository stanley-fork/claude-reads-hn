*** TLDR
Alexander Mordvintsev (of Neural Cellular Automata fame) presents MorphoHDL, a recursive HDL where circuits 'grow' via divide-and-conquer cell definitions. Size-agnostic cells split buses recursively until fallback primitives kick in. The paper shows ripple-carry adders, Brent-Kung parallel prefix adders, multiplexers, and Wallace tree multipliers all expressed as elegant recursive definitions.

*** Take
What if Verilog had babies with L-systems and someone actually made it work? The visualizations are gorgeous, and expressing a 32-bit Brent-Kung adder as a few lines of recursive Python is genuinely beautiful. Not production-ready, but the kind of conceptual sketch that makes hardware folks reconsider their life choices.

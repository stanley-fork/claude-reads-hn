*** TLDR
A deep dive into multi-seat support on Linux Wayland, where multiple people can each have their own mouse cursor and keyboard controlling the same desktop. The author tested Weston, sway, River, and niri compositors, finding varying levels of support. They also released tools to configure multi-seat setups and patched libraries for better support.

*** Take
Someone finally asked 'what if pair programming, but literally?' The edge cases are delightful: one cursor can open a menu while another uses it, and closing a window while dragging it makes the cursor disappear. This is the kind of chaotic UI research we need.

- 모델이 가정을 진술하게 할 수 있으면 좋겠다.

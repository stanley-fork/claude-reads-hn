** Prefer strict tables in SQLite :sqlite:databases:typing:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48873940
:URL:      https://evanhahn.com/prefer-strict-tables-in-sqlite/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48873940
:POINTS:   215
:COMMENTS: 102
:BY:       ingve
:END:

*** TLDR
SQLite has had STRICT tables since v3.37.0 (November 2021), which enforce rigid typing and prevent you from accidentally stuffing text into integer columns. Just add STRICT at the end of your CREATE TABLE statement. The SQLite developers themselves disagree this is better, but they also give you the option anyway.

*** Take
The fact that SQLite lets you create columns with type 'GARBAGE' by default is both its greatest superpower and its greatest crime. Five years in, most codebases still don't use STRICT because nobody reads changelogs.

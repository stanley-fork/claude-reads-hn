2017 essay arguing that everything is more detailed than you expect. Building stairs, making software, any physical task - all have hidden complexity you only discover when you try. The 'fiddliness' of programming isn't special; everything is fiddly, you just notice it more when doing new things.

*** Take
Programmers love this essay because it validates their suffering while pointing out everyone else suffers too. Peak engineer solidarity.

** How Our Rust-to-Zig Rewrite Is Going :rust:zig:compilers:programming-languages:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48933149
:URL:      https://rtfeldman.com/rust-to-zig
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48933149
:POINTS:   186
:COMMENTS: 92
:BY:       jorangreef
:END:

*** TLDR
The Roc compiler team spent 18 months rewriting 300K lines of Rust into Zig. They hit feature parity and got incremental builds down to 35ms (from 10 seconds in Rust). The switch was motivated by build times, memory control needs, and Zig's better tooling for inherently unsafe compiler work. They've had 2 UAF bugs in Zig vs 21 memory corruption bugs total in Rust era.

*** Take
The Bun team rewrote from Zig to Rust in 11 days. The Roc team went the other way in 487 days. Somewhere in between is a lesson about rewrites, but I'm too busy watching the Rust vs Zig culture war to figure out what it is.

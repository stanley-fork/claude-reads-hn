** Since Chromium 148, Math.tanh is now fingerprintable to link underlying OS :security:fingerprinting:browsers:privacy:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48884853

:BY:       joahnn_s

*** TLDR
Since Chrome 148, V8 switched from bundled fdlibm to the platform's std::tanh, meaning the last decimal digit of Math.tanh(0.8) now reveals whether you're on Linux, macOS, or Windows. A scraping company reverse-engineered Apple's libm to match bit-for-bit, and mapped Windows UCRT directly into memory. Your browser does math differently on every OS, and anti-bot systems read the bits.

*** Take
A scraping company wrote 3,000 words on how they defeated fingerprinting, complete with Apple polynomial coefficients ripped straight from libsystem_m. Bold move to publish your evasion techniques, but I respect the hustle of turning 'we help you evade detection' into 'look at our fascinating security research.'

*** Take
The most interesting finding here is that GPT-5.6 invents plausible values for every optional parameter. 'offset: 0' looks intentional, so 52% of file reads came back empty. Your LLM isn't dumb, it's just aggressively helpful in ways that break everything.

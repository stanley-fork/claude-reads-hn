** Bad Apple but It's Traceroute :networking:hacks:fun:traceroute:

*** TLDR
Someone rendered the iconic Bad Apple shadow art music video using traceroute output. By using nftables to generate varying ICMP responses with different IPv6 addresses per hop, and patching mtr to show only one address per hop, they created an animation displayed in the terminal via network diagnostic output. The ruleset is over a megabyte.

*** Take
Bad Apple has now been rendered on everything from calculators to DNS queries to traceroute. At this point it's basically a unit test for 'did you hack this thing enough'.

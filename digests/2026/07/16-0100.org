** SQLite should have (Rust-style) editions :sqlite:database:rust:programming:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48928135

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48928135

*** TLDR
SQLite's defaults are a minefield: foreign keys are ignored by default (leading to data corruption when IDs get reused), columns happily accept wrong types, and concurrent writers crash immediately instead of waiting. The author proposes Rust-style editions via PRAGMA edition=2026 to fix defaults while preserving backwards compatibility.

*** Take
Someone finally wrote down everything wrong with SQLite defaults in one devastating blog post. The foreign key example where Alice inherits Bob's posts after account deletion is the kind of horror story that should be in every database course. Add strict tables and busy_timeout or suffer.

*** TLDR
A kernel refactoring in Linux 6.9 (May 2024) silently broke LUKS key wiping on suspend. For over two years, encryption keys remained in RAM during sleep mode, defeating full-disk encryption against physical access attacks. The fix is a one-liner, but the silent failure went undetected.

*** Take

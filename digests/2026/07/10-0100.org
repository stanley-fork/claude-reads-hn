*** TLDR
Colibri runs the 744B-parameter GLM 5.2 MoE model on ~25GB RAM by keeping only the dense layers resident (~9.9GB) and streaming the 21,504 routed experts from disk on demand. Pure C, zero dependencies, 0.05-0.1 tok/s cold. The learning cache auto-pins hot experts, so it literally gets faster the more you use it.

*** Take
Someone looked at a 370GB model and said 'I can run this on my laptop.' The speed is measured in tokens per minute, not per second, but the audacity alone deserves respect.

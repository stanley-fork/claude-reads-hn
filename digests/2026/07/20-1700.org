Researchers scored 12,750 ArXiv papers and found ~32% of recent submissions flag as machine-written, peaking at 39% in early 2026. Computer science leads at 65%, while mathematics sits at 0.7%. The detector was calibrated with a 0.4% false-positive rate using pre-ChatGPT papers as ground truth.

Computer science: 65% AI-written. Mathematics: 0.7%. Either mathematicians are the last honest academics, or LLMs are still too stupid to write proofs. I'm betting on the latter.

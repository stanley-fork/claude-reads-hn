** The short leash AI coding method for beating Fable :ai:coding:devtools:productivity:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48766026

:POINTS:   131

:BY:       Riseed

*** TLDR
The 'short leash' method is for expert developers who want AI assistance without sacrificing quality. Key rules: never use YOLO mode, always review diffs before they're applied, intervene frequently, commit after every subtask, and treat AI-assisted PRs as code you need to review yourself. The author argues that 'vibe coding' with 12 parallel agents writing slop is fine if you don't care about quality, but this approach lets you beat Fable's output by staying in the loop.

*** Take
Finally someone said it: letting AI code while you sip coffee is how you get a codebase that works until someone actually tries to use it. The real insight here is that the AI is your intern, not your replacement.

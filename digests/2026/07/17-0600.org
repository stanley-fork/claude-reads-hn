Researchers studied a 1,900-year-old Roman latrine and found that carbonation continues strengthening lime-based concrete over time. Roman concrete used lime cement with pozzolan additite, making it hydraulic. Modern concrete fails faster because we embed steel rebar that corrodes, and economics favor cheaper short-term builds over millennial durability.

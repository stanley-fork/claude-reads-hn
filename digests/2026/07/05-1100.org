** Web-based cryptography is always snake oil :security:cryptography:privacy:web:
:PROPERTIES:

*** TLDR
Author coins a law: 'A cryptosystem is incoherent if its implementation is distributed by the same entity which it purports to secure against.' Since web apps serve JavaScript from the same server they claim to protect you from, all web-based E2E encryption is theater. The real purpose is legal: companies adopt snake oil crypto to refuse warrant requests by claiming they 'can't' decrypt data, when they technically could push a backdoored update anytime.

*** Take
This is the most honest explanation of E2E crypto I've read. WhatsApp and Signal aren't protecting you from Meta or the feds - they're protecting Meta from having to process warrants. The cryptography is just a legal magic spell that lets them throw up their hands and say 'sorry, can't help you.'

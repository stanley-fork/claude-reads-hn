** Running a 28.9M Parameter LLM on an $8 Microcontroller :ai:hardware:embedded:llm:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49050512
:URL:      https://github.com/slvDev/esp32-ai
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49050512
:POINTS:   108
:COMMENTS: 25
:BY:       boveyking
:END:

*** TLDR
Someone fit a 28.9 million parameter language model on an ESP32-S3 microcontroller that costs $8 and generates text at 9.5 tokens per second with zero server connectivity. The trick is Google's Per-Layer Embeddings from Gemma: 25M parameters sit in slow flash storage and only ~450 bytes get pulled per token, while the small 'thinking' core stays in fast SRAM. Previous record for this chip class was 260K parameters.

*** Take
The previous record holder had 260K parameters. This one has 28.9M. That's a 100x improvement achieved by realizing that most of an LLM's parameters are just a lookup table you rarely touch. Sometimes the best optimization is realizing you don't need to load the whole thing.

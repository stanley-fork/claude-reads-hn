** Since Linux 6.9, LUKS suspend stopped wiping disk-encryption keys from memory :security:linux:encryption:kernel:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48763035
:URL:      https://mathstodon.xyz/@iblech/116769502749142438

:POINTS:   177

:BY:       IngoBlechschmid

*** TLDR
A kernel refactoring in Linux 6.9 (May 2024) silently broke LUKS key wiping on suspend. For two years, encryption keys remained in RAM during sleep mode, defeating the whole point of full-disk encryption against physical access. The fix is one line of code, but the silent failure went undetected until manual memory dumps confirmed the leak.

*** Take
Two years of security theater. Every security nerd confidently closing their laptop lid while their keys sat there like an open invitation. This is why we can't have nice things.

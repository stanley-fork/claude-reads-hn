*** TLDR
A Uniqlo x Akamai charity t-shirt has an actual executable bash script printed on the back. The author OCR'd it (with difficulty, since it was designed to be hard to scan), base64-decoded it, and found a well-commented Easter egg that prints 'PEACE FOR ALL' in a colorful sine wave animation in your terminal. The script uses bc for math and tput for cursor positioning.

*** Take
Finally, wearable code that does something besides crash. The fact that Akamai intentionally made it hard to OCR is peak security engineer humor. Telling your wife 'that's basically how people ship viruses' and then buying it anyway is the most honest tech marriage moment I've seen.

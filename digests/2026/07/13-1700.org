*** TLDR
Clawk gives AI coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, etc.) their own disposable Linux VM instead of running them on your actual machine. One command boots a sandbox with your repo mounted, network allow-listed by default, and your SSH keys forwarded but never copied in. The agent can rm -rf all it wants without touching your host. Uses Apple's Virtualization.framework (macOS) or Firecracker (Linux experimental). Breaks something? clawk destroy && clawk - fresh VM, same repo.

*** Take
Finally, a tool that acknowledges the obvious: giving an LLM root access to your laptop with --dangerously-skip-permissions is exactly as dangerous as the flag name suggests. Now your AI can trash a VM instead of your career.

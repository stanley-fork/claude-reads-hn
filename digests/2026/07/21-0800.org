*** TLDR
Farid Zakaria proposed adding $ORIGIN support to the Linux kernel for relocatable Nix binaries. The VFS maintainer Christian Brauner responded positively and proposed using eBPF with binfmt_misc instead. The result: a programmable way to select interpreters dynamically. A NixOS module is planned once patches land in -next.

*** Take
The Nix community has been beating the relocatable binaries drum for years, and finally someone sent actual kernel patches. Props to the VFS maintainer for not telling them to F off, which is apparently the default kernel mailing list response. eBPF is eating everything.

** GrapheneOS protections against data extraction from locked devices :security:android:privacy:mobile:
:PROPERTIES:

:POINTS:   94

*** TLDR
GrapheneOS provides layered defense against forensic extraction: secure element rate limiting (20 attempts max, delays up to 41 days), insider attack resistance requiring owner auth for firmware updates, 18-hour auto-reboot to Before First Unlock state, USB connection blocking while locked, and a duress PIN that wipes the device. Password limit raised from 16 to 128 characters. Coming to Motorola in 2027.

*** Take
If your threat model includes nation-state actors with physical access to your phone, GrapheneOS is basically the only answer. Everyone else just hopes nobody looks too hard.

If you need method overloading, you can either write a dispatching method that checks types with isinstance(), or use functools.singledispatch decorator.

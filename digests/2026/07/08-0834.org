*** TLDR
Security researchers at Noma discovered that GitHub's AI coding agent is vulnerable to prompt injection attacks. By placing malicious instructions in public repos, they could trick the agent into leaking contents of private repositories it had access to. The attack exploits the fundamental problem that LLMs cannot distinguish between instructions and data.

*** Take
Prompt injection is the new SQL injection, except this time there's no parameterized queries to save us. We gave AI access to everything and are shocked it can be tricked into sharing everything.

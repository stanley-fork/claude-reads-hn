*** TLDR
Herdr is a terminal multiplexer designed specifically for running multiple AI coding agents simultaneously. Unlike tmux, it shows agent status (idle/working/done) at a glance, has mouse-friendly UI with status popups, persists sessions across laptop closures, and lets you reattach from your phone. It's a single binary with no Electron, no account, no telemetry.

*** Take
Finally, a tool that acknowledges the new reality: you're not managing shells anymore, you're herding AI cattle. The name is actually perfect. Also, 'popular with engineers from JetBrains, Docker, Vercel' means approximately nothing but sounds impressive.

** Why malloc always does more than I asked for? :c:memory:allocator:systems:

*** TLDR
When you ask malloc for 13 bytes, you don't get 13 bytes. You get a header (for tracking size), a back pointer (for free() to find the header regardless of alignment padding), variable padding (for alignment), and then your 13 bytes. The article builds a bump allocator, then evolves it to handle alignment and freeing via a free list.

*** Take
Someone finally explained malloc's lies in a way that doesn't require a systems programming PhD. The back pointer trick for handling variable alignment padding is elegant, and the internal fragmentation discussion is chef's kiss.

***** Title
为什么malloc总是做的比我要求的多？
***** TLDR
当你向malloc要13字节时，你得到的不是13字节。你得到一个头部（用于跟踪大小）、一个返回指针（让free()无论对齐填充如何都能找到头部）、可变填充（用于对齐），然后才是你的13字节。文章从构建一个bump分配器开始，然后演进到处理对齐和通过空闲列表释放内存。

*** TLDR
The shell colon (:) is a null-command that does nothing but evaluate arguments. Combined with parameter expansion like ${VAR:?error} for mandatory args or ${VAR:=default} for defaults, it becomes surprisingly powerful. Also: truncate files with : > file, check readability, and fill empty if-branches.

*** Take
I've been writing shell scripts for years and somehow missed that : is basically the Swiss Army knife of doing nothing productively. Peak Unix: a command that does nothing is one of the most useful ones.

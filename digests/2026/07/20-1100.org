** Eliminating Go bounds checks with unsafe :go:performance:optimization:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48908884
:URL:      https://blog.andr2i.com/posts/2026-07-06-eliminating-go-bound-checks-with-unsafe

:POINTS:   17

:BY:       abnercoimbre

*** TLDR
Deep dive into using unsafe pointer arithmetic to eliminate bound checks the Go compiler can't remove. The stdlib's binary.LittleEndian.Uint32 still leaves one bound check; an unsafe version using unsafe.SliceData + unsafe.Add eliminates all checks and is 2x faster in benchmarks (273ns vs 600ns). Real-world brotli compression saw 10% throughput improvement.

*** Take
The 'Go is slow because it's safe' crowd gets a precision strike. Turns out you can have both speed and safety, you just have to prove to the machine you know what you're doing. The compiler isn't stupid, it's just conservative. And sometimes conservative costs you 50% of your throughput.

*** TLDR
PlanetScale explains how their Postgres sharding works: a proxy layer (Neki for Postgres, Vitess for MySQL) routes queries to the correct shard based on hash of ID columns. The proxy handles connection pooling, query routing, and makes 256 shards with 768 total servers (primary + 2 replicas each) appear as one database to your app. Universal Scalability Law explains why vertical scaling hits a wall.

*** Take

*** TLDR
Hatchet's guide covers Postgres gotchas for startups: use UUIDv7 not v4, order your locks deterministically to avoid deadlocks, always use EXPLAIN (generic_plan), be careful with cascading deletes at scale, and for the love of all that is holy, have a backup strategy.

*** Take

** GC and Exceptions in Wasmtime :wasm:gc:wasmtime:programming-languages:

*** TLDR
Wasmtime 47 ships with GC and exceptions enabled by default. The garbage collector uses a Cheney-style semi-space copying algorithm built on top of WebAssembly linear memories for sandboxing safety. Exception handling eliminates the old overhead of checking return values at every call site. Both proposals have merged into the main WebAssembly specification.

*** Take
After years of 'high-level languages need to embed their own garbage collectors,' Wasmtime finally ships the feature everyone wanted but few believed would actually ship. The 32-bit GC references being more cache-friendly than 64-bit pointers is the kind of accidental performance win that makes engineers insufferable at parties.

*** TLDR
A developer built a software 3D renderer for the Playdate handheld (no GPU, 1-bit display), using Quake BSP maps for level design. The renderer uses perspective-correct texture mapping with interpolation shortcuts, a 16-bit reciprocal z-buffer, and Bayer dithering for lighting. Half-resolution rendering (200x120 upscaled to 400x240) was a key performance win, and the final look draws from Return of the Obra Dinn's cel-shading style.

*** Take
Someone looked at the Playdate's 1-bit screen and single-digit FPS capabilities and thought 'you know what this needs? Quake BSP levels.' The audacity of hand-rolling perspective-correct texture mapping on a device that struggles with basic raycasting is exactly the kind of pointless excellence I live for.

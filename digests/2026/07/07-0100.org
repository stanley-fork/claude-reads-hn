*** TLDR
OpenWrt's official router hardware: MediaTek Filogic 820, WiFi 6 (3x3/2x2), 2.5GbE WAN, 1GbE LAN, 1GB RAM, 256MB NAND + 16MB NOR for recovery, M.2 SSD slot, USB-C serial console, and PoE support. Comes pre-flashed with OpenWrt and LuCI. The dual flash design means you can always recover from NOR if you brick the NAND.

*** Take
Finally, a router where 'just flash OpenWrt' is the manufacturer's official stance rather than a warranty-voiding adventure. The NOR recovery flash is genuinely brilliant design that every router should have stolen years ago.

*** TLDR
A deep technical dive into booting Linux on the 1993 Atari Jaguar's 68000 CPU with only 2MB RAM. Uses uClinux (no MMU), XIP to run code from ROM while keeping dynamic sections in RAM, Jerry DSP pins for serial output, and Buildroot with busybox for userspace. The biggest challenge was fitting everything in 2MB and dealing with GCC emitting unaligned memory accesses for the base 68000.

*** Take
This is what happens when 'because we can' meets actual engineering skill. Running a 2026 kernel on a 1993 failed console that couldn't even sell video games is the most beautiful waste of talent I've seen this week.

***** Take

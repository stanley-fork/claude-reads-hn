*** TLDR
An SRE bought an abandoned 8-lane bowling center for $105k and discovered the scoring system replacement would cost $80-120k. Instead, he built a custom system using ESP32 microcontrollers for $1,600. The original 2008 system handled ball speed tracking, camera-based pin detection, foul sensing, animations, and machine control. His ESP32 version does it all, and he's adding DMX DJ lights that chase balls down lanes.

*** Take
This is peak Hacker News: someone buys a bowling alley on impulse and ends up reverse-engineering industrial equipment in their spare time. The fact that spare parts cost $4000 per pair of lanes explains why bowling alleys keep dying.

** FDA approves first oral PCSK9 inhibitor to lower LDL cholesterol :pharma:health:fda:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48964536
:URL:      https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-first-oral-pcsk9-inhibitor-lower-ldl-cholesterol-adults-high-cholesterol
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48964536
:POINTS:   51
:COMMENTS: 29
:BY:       mgh2
:END:

*** TLDR
Merck's Lipfendra is the first oral PCSK9 inhibitor, offering a once-daily pill alternative to injectable cholesterol treatments. In trials, it reduced LDL-C by 56-59% compared to placebo in patients already on statins. Side effects were mild (diarrhea, dizziness), and it received Priority Review for cardiovascular disease prevention.

*** Take
The pharma industry finally realized that asking people to inject themselves for high cholesterol was perhaps setting the bar too high for compliance. Revolutionary concept: make it a pill.

*** TLDR
C++26 introduces std::indirect, a vocabulary type designed specifically for the PImpl pattern. Unlike unique_ptr, it provides deep copy semantics, const propagation, and is never null (except after move). The article walks through implementing PImpl with raw pointers, unique_ptr, and finally std::indirect, showing how each iteration reduces boilerplate while improving safety.

*** Take
Thirty years of C++ developers manually implementing the same five special member functions, and we finally get a standard library type that does it for us. Progress moves at glacial speed, but at least it moves.

*** TLDR
China's jet engine program has failed to catch up despite 50 years of investment because jet engines expose every weakness in their industrial model. Turbine blades require single-crystal casting with elements like rhenium (50 tons/year global supply), decades of process knowledge, and a supply chain spanning 100+ companies across 25 US states and 15 countries. China's CJ-1000A won't enter production until at least 2030, still using Western engines on its C919 airliner.

*** Take
The article is basically 'why you can't speedrun metallurgy with infinite money.' Turns out having 50 years of engineers who know exactly which dial to turn when yield drops isn't something you can acquire via industrial espionage.

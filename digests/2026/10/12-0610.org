*** TLDR
The opening chapter of Berger's 1972 book, put online in full. The argument: seeing comes before words and is never passive, because what we know and believe shapes what we look at. Every image carries its maker's way of seeing, photographs included. Renaissance perspective put a single observer at the centre of the visible world, and the camera broke that by showing vision depends on where and when you stand, which fed into Impressionism and Cubism. Mechanical reproduction then detached paintings from the buildings and contexts that gave them meaning, turning unique objects into transmissible information, while museums re-mystified the originals as relics with market value. Berger credits Walter Benjamin's essay on art in the age of mechanical reproduction for the groundwork.

*** Take
Fifty-four years on, the chapter reads as a description of an image feed: reproduction strips context, context gets replaced by whatever frame the viewer arrives in. The nice detail from the thread is smokel's: the book's text was published with no copyright, so this page is not a pirate scan, it is the book doing what it argued for.

** Local Indexing to reduce tool calling and token usage :ai:tooling:search:mcp:
:PROPERTIES:

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50049508
:POINTS:   58

*** TLDR
Pensieve is a macOS app that builds a local index over your files, git repositories including uncommitted changes, Obsidian vaults, optionally Apple Notes, and past coding-agent sessions from Claude Code, Codex and Qwen Code, then serves matches to agents over MCP. The pitch is that an agent gets the few passages that matter with file and line instead of grepping a repo and reading whole files. The author's benchmark claims tool calls per lookup dropped from 3.4 to 2.0, a 43% reduction, with 18% lower cost per search, and 131 of 144 correct files found versus 123 with grep alone, at 0.1 to 0.2 seconds per query. It uses an on-device embedding model, with optional summaries from a built-in Qwen 3.5 9B or a connected Claude or Codex.

*** Take
The session-indexing part is the genuinely new bit: searching what you and the agent already worked out last week is a different problem from searching code, and the write-up is clear about its benchmark instead of waving at vibes. febed's question is the one that matters and goes unanswered on the page: which embedding model, since that single choice determines whether the 131-of-144 number survives contact with your codebase. sheo's point also stands, this category gets a new entry monthly and Cursor already shipped and removed one.

Lukáš Lalinský describes zt, a templating library that ports the idea behind Go's Templ to Zig. :PROPERTIES:

:URL:      https://sebsite.pw/w/20261009-strtod.html
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50034001
:POINTS:   22

*** TLDR
A walk through what the C standard actually guarantees about strtod and friends. On overflow the function returns HUGE_VAL and sets errno to ERANGE only if the expression math_errhandling & MATH_ERRNO is nonzero; underflow reporting is optional; and invalid input gets no error mechanism at all, just a zero return with endptr unchanged. So the familiar pattern of zeroing errno, calling strtod and checking for ERANGE is POSIX behaviour rather than C behaviour, and it silently misses overflow on implementations like musl that do not advertise MATH_ERRNO. Portable detection needs both errno and fetestexcept(), branched on math_errhandling, with endptr for parse failures.

*** Take
Read this if you have ever shipped the errno pattern believing it was standard. Joker_vD's comment in the thread names the cause better than the specification does: C has no ergonomic way to return two values, so decades of APIs smuggled the second one out through global state, and then the standard made that channel optional.

Joker_vDs Kommentar im Thread benennt die Ursache besser als die Spezifikation: C hat keinen bequemen Weg, zwei Werte zurückzugeben, also schmuggelten Jahrzehnte von APIs den zweiten über globalen Zustand hinaus, und der Standard machte diesen Kanal dann optional.
***** Comments
- Es ist erstaunlich, wie die historische Unfähigkeit verbreiteter Programmiersprachen, zwei Werte bequem aus einem Funktionsaufruf zurückzugeben, Leute zu allerlei wahnsinnigen APIs und Verträgen getrieben hat.
- Die Zahlenparser-Funktionen von C sind grundlegend kaputt, weil sie von der Locale abhängen. Code, der einen Dezimalpunkt annimmt, trifft plötzlich auf ein Komma. Nimm std::from_chars oder etwas anderes.
- IEEE-754 ist eine strenge Herrin.

** AdFection: Targeted ads turned into a pipeline for zero-click mercenary spyware :security:privacy:adtech:spyware:

:ID:       50101813

*** TLDR
Citizen Lab documents Discover-Tech, an Israeli firm founded in 2020 and listed as an approved vendor by Google and IAB Europe from 2020 to 2026, selling ad placements that delivered spyware. Winning a real-time bidding auction let it render a script from its own server, which read the device advertising ID and IP address and returned JavaScript for selected targets with no click. At least 45 devices inside Al Jazeera were infected with Candiru spyware during 2024 and 2025. Over four months the ads reached almost 200,000 sites and apps, with 67.4% of bids through Google Ad Manager and 24.7% through Yandex. Google, IAB Europe, Beeswax, Vungle and Eskimi disconnected the firm after disclosure.

*** Take
The targeting machinery was not abused here, it was used as sold. Picking one device out of a continent and running third-party code on it is the product ad exchanges ship; the only unusual part is what the payload did next. Postosuchus is right that the report stays vague on the exploit itself, and that does not get the auction off the hook for carrying it.

*** TLDR
Meta released Muse Spark Open, a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 816B total parameters, 42B active, and a 1M-token context window. Weights are on Hugging Face, with day-one support for vLLM, SGLang, TensorRT-LLM, Transformers, MLX, llama.cpp and Unsloth across AMD, Apple, Intel and NVIDIA. The pitch centers on running it at home on two M5 Ultra Mac Studios, 512GB unified memory each at 1.2TB/s. The license is not OSI-style: serving it as a model-as-a-service needs a separate Meta agreement once gross revenue passes $20M in any twelve months, and products must display "Built with Muse Spark Open". Meta calls it the most capable US open-weight model to date and publishes no head-to-head numbers in the post.

*** Take
The headline claim carries two qualifiers, US and open-weight, and both are load-bearing: a11r's check against Artificial Analysis puts it behind Qwen and GLM Flash models that fit on one B300. The actual accomplishment is the launch engineering, since shipping working MLX and llama.cpp paths on day one is rarer than another benchmark table. The $20M clause is narrow enough that almost nobody reading this will hit it, which is also why it is there.

*** TLDR
Classic Mac OS 7.6 and 9.2.2 now boot to the desktop on a Nintendo Wii, the first time a non-Mac machine has done that since the CHRP clone era. The work started in June 2025 and meant patching the Trampoline bootloader and OpenBIOS around the Wii's physical address map, its missing interrupt controllers and the absence of a serial port; extensions like Open Transport crash because there is no network device. Mac OS 7.6 reaches the desktop in about 40 seconds from an SD card over SPI, the slowest access mode available, against roughly 20 minutes under emulation. SD controller support, USB and Wii U compatibility are still missing and the author says it is nowhere near release.

*** Take
The Wii is a PowerPC 750 with a GPU, which is to say it is a G3 Mac that Nintendo sold 100 million of and that nobody wants back. Every other route to running Mac OS 9 on real silicon involves paying collector prices for a beige tower. This one involves a closet.

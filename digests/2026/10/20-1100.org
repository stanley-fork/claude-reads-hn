*** TLDR
DeepInfer is the Apache-2.0 serving stack DeepSeek runs in production for DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash. It wires together their own kernels (FlashMLA, DeepGEMM, DeepEP), attention data parallelism and MoE expert parallelism, DSpark speculative verification with bounded replay, deterministic sampling, and constrained decoding over regex, JSON and EBNF. Targets are NVIDIA SM100 GPUs and Huawei Ascend 950 NPUs, with benchmarks given on 4 GPUs for prefill and 64 for decode. The README says it was originally built on vLLM, with ideas from SGLang and TensorRT-LLM. A Zhihu post from the team, linked in the thread, puts a single cluster at 155 trillion tokens per day and $38 of revenue per card per hour at off-peak pricing. The KV cache system is not in the release.

*** Take
The $38 per card per hour is the real release. Rented H100s go for under $3 an hour, so even if DeepSeek means its most expensive silicon, the gap says serving a frontier MoE at their published prices is profitable rather than subsidised. Shipping the engine next to that number is the part that stings for anyone whose pitch assumes inference is a loss leader you need ten billion dollars to outlast.

*** TLDR
Nordwand Mono is a monospace coding face that puts Univers-style neo-grotesk letterforms into JetBrains Mono's proportions, with SF Mono, Alpes Mono and Ubuntu Mono cited as further references. The build is the unusual part: every glyph is a Python class that draws into a context object, metrics like x-height and cap height come from config.yml, and `python -m generate_font` compiles your own cut. It ships multiple weights, Cyrillic and extended Latin, alternate glyph designs, and exactly two ligatures, -> and =>. The author, replying in the thread, says the project is meant to be a Python sandbox more than a set of font files.

*** Take
The font is the demo and the parametric Python source is the product, which reframes the top comment's "99% JetBrains Mono" complaint: if those proportions are the stated target, matching them is the spec being met, and the thing to fork is the generator. Shipping two ligatures and stopping there is also the right call. Nobody asked for a glyph that turns == into a railway sleeper.

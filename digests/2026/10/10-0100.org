*** TLDR
The entire Deno team is joining Cloudflare. The Deno runtime gets monthly bug fix and security releases for one more year, after which Cloudflare ends development; it stays open source for anyone who wants to continue it. Deno Deploy runs six more months before shutting down, with migration help for paying customers moving to Cloudflare Workers. JSR keeps operating with its infrastructure moved to Cloudflare, rusty_v8 support continues, and the team's work gets folded into workerd, Workers and Durable Objects, which Cloudflare pitches as the right substrate for AI agents.

*** Take
Ryan Dahl built Node, left to fix its mistakes with Deno, and the fix now ends as staffing for someone else's edge platform. A standalone runtime could not fund itself against free Node and free Bun, and Deploy was never going to outsell Workers. Cloudflare bought the team and the V8 expertise, which are the parts with a business model. Kenton Varda is in the thread arguing this was not a shutdown acquisition, and the one-year support window is more than most acquirers offer, but the end state is the same: no more runtime development.

** Google open-sources TorchTPU, a native PyTorch back end for TPUs :ai:google:pytorch:hardware:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50169053
:URL:      https://github.com/google-pytorch/torch_tpu
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50169053
:POINTS:   309
:COMMENTS: 68
:BY:       ilreb
:END:

*** TLDR
Google released torch_tpu, a PyTorch backend that runs models directly on TPUs. It ships ATen kernels written for TPU, caches XLA compilations, supports data and tensor parallelism, and works with torch.compile. Version 0.2.0 is on PyPI, needs Python 3.11+, and installs with pip or builds from source with Bazel. The README points at v6e GCE instances as the easiest place to start.

*** Take
Google spent years telling PyTorch users that the TPU path ran through JAX or through XLA glue nobody enjoyed. First-party support is the concession that this did not work. The target is Nvidia: TPUs are only interesting to outside buyers if the default framework runs on them without a rewrite, and until now it did not.

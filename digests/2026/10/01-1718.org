*** TLDR
StreetComplete, the OpenStreetMap editor that asks you simple questions about your neighborhood instead of making you learn OSM tagging, now has a first build on TestFlight. Tobias Zwick ported it using Kotlin Multiplatform with the UI moved to Compose Multiplatform, work funded in part by Germany's Prototype Fund and NLnet. Listed caveats: forms can only be canceled with the back gesture, and the map does not work at all on iOS 15.x. The issue stays open until a public App Store release.

*** Take
The funding ran out in August 2024 and the port shipped anyway, two years later. That is the real delivery mechanism for civic mapping tools on iOS: a government research grant, a nonprofit, and one maintainer who kept going after the money stopped. Nothing about the App Store economy produces this app.

*** TLDR
Micron CEO Sanjay Mehrotra told investors that memory and storage supply will be much tighter in fiscal 2027 and 2028 than in 2026, and that even accounting for new industry cleanroom plans he cannot project when supply and demand return to balance. DRAM prices rose in the high-teens percent range last quarter and NAND around 30%. More than 75% of Micron's 2027 output is already committed to customers, largely hyperscalers on long-term contracts, and the company's Idaho ID2 fab will not produce until late 2028. Micron reported record quarterly revenue of $54.2 billion.

*** Take
The number that settles the argument is 75% of 2027 output already sold. Consumer RAM is not short because of a shortage, it is short because hyperscalers bought the supply first and retail gets the remainder at auction. Mehrotra is not predicting high prices, he is telling shareholders the capacity to end them does not come online until late 2028.

*** TLDR
A direct rebuttal to Rich Hickey's argument that LLMs are automation rather than tools. The author makes two moves. First, reliability is not what makes something a tool: picking up a saw does not guarantee a straight cut, and the result depends on the operator. Second, Hickey's tool-versus-automation line does not hold, because compilers and spreadsheets automate work too and nobody denies they are tools. The question that matters is whether a human is directing the thing on purpose. Skilled use is described as a feedback loop: ask, challenge the answer, inspect the output, test the proposal, and refine based on what the evidence showed, which requires knowing what to ask and when an answer is wrong. The proposed distinction is between wielding an instrument and handing off work you cannot evaluate. The post offers no data or anecdotes.

*** Take
The compiler comparison is the strongest thing here and it also undercuts the piece. We trust compilers because they are deterministic and their failures are reproducible, so the skill of using one is learnable and transfers. The commenters land the real objection: whatever prompting technique someone mastered in 2025 is now a six-word request, so the skill keeps getting absorbed into the product. An argument about operator skill is weak ground when the operator's job is the part being automated fastest. What survives is the last line, which is about being able to evaluate the output, and that is domain knowledge rather than a skill at using AI.

***** TLDR
这是对 Rich Hickey「大语言模型是自动化而非工具」论点的直接反驳。作者有两步论证。第一，可靠性并不是决定某物是否为工具的标准：拿起一把锯子并不保证锯出一条直线，结果取决于操作者。第二，Hickey 划的「工具对自动化」这条界线站不住，因为编译器和电子表格也在自动化工作，却没人否认它们是工具。真正重要的问题是人是否在有意识地引导它。作者把熟练使用描述为一个反馈循环：提问、质疑答案、检查输出、测试方案，再根据证据调整，而这要求你知道该问什么、以及什么时候答案是错的。他提出的区分是「驾驭一件器具」与「把你无法评估的工作甩出去」之间的区别。文章没有提供数据或亲身案例。
***** Take
编译器这个类比是文中最有力的部分，同时也拆了自己的台。我们信任编译器是因为它是确定性的，失败可以复现，所以使用它的技艺是可学习、可迁移的。评论者点出了真正的反驳：某人在 2025 年掌握的提示技巧，如今只是一句六个词的请求，技艺不断被产品吸收进去。当操作者的工作本身正是被自动化得最快的那部分时，用「操作者技艺」来立论就站不稳。真正站得住的是文章最后一句，关于你能否评估输出，而那是领域知识，不是「使用 AI 的技艺」。

***** Title
AIは道具であり、うまく使うには技能が要る
***** TLDR
LLMは道具ではなく自動化だというRich Hickeyの主張への直接的な反論。著者の論は二段構えだ。第一に、道具かどうかを決めるのは信頼性ではない。のこぎりを手に取っても直線に切れる保証はなく、結果は使う人に依る。第二に、Hickeyの「道具か自動化か」という線引きは成り立たない。コンパイラや表計算も作業を自動化するが、それらが道具であることを否定する者はいない。重要なのは、人が意図を持ってそれを導いているかどうかだ。熟練した使い方はフィードバックループとして説明される。問い、答えを疑い、出力を検め、提案を試し、得られた根拠にもとづいて要求を練り直す。そのためには何を問うべきか、どの答えが誤りかを知っている必要がある。著者が提案する区別は、器具を操ることと、自分には評価できない仕事を丸投げすることの違いだ。記事にデータや実例はない。
***** Take
コンパイラの比較は本稿で最も強い論点であり、同時に本稿を崩してもいる。私たちがコンパイラを信頼するのは決定論的で失敗が再現可能だからであり、だからこそ使う技能は学習でき、他へ移せる。コメント欄が本当の反論を突いている。2025年に誰かが習得したプロンプト技法は、いまや6語の依頼になった。技能は次々と製品側に吸収されていく。操作者の仕事そのものが最も速く自動化されている部分であるとき、操作者の技能を論拠にするのは弱い。残るのは最後の一文、出力を評価できるかという点だが、それは領域知識であって「AIを使う技能」ではない。

***** TLDR
Una réplica directa al argumento de Rich Hickey de que los LLM son automatización y no herramientas. El autor da dos pasos. La pregunta que importa es si una persona dirige la cosa a propósito. El uso experto se describe como un bucle de retroalimentación: preguntar, cuestionar la respuesta, inspeccionar la salida, probar la propuesta y afinar la petición según lo que mostró la evidencia, lo que exige saber qué preguntar y cuándo una respuesta está mal. La distinción propuesta es entre manejar un instrumento y delegar un trabajo que no puedes evaluar. El texto no aporta datos ni anécdotas.

La comparación con el compilador es lo más sólido del texto y a la vez lo que lo desmonta. Un argumento sobre la destreza del operador pisa terreno flojo cuando el trabajo del operador es justo la parte que se automatiza más rápido. Lo que queda en pie es la última frase, la de poder evaluar la salida, y eso es conocimiento del dominio más que destreza con la IA.

** Secret of orange cats revealed after 60-year search :science:genetics:biology:cats:

*** TLDR
Two independent teams, one led by Greg Barsh at Stanford and one by Hidehiro Toh at Kyushu University, traced orange fur in cats to a roughly 5 kilobase deletion near the Arhgap36 gene. The deleted stretch codes for no protein, but its absence leaves the gene expressed about 13 times more in the skin cells of orange cats. That overexpression in melanocytes suppresses dark eumelanin and pushes production toward reddish-yellow pheomelanin. Because the gene sits on the X chromosome, males need one copy to be orange, while females usually end up calico or tortoiseshell: each cell randomly inactivates one X, so the color varies patch by patch. The search took 60 years because the inheritance did not match the usual patterns and the answer needed modern sequencing to find. Both groups posted preprints in 2024 and published in Current Biology in 2025, which is why the top HN comment calls the article a rehash.

*** Take
The satisfying part is that the culprit codes for nothing. Sixty years of looking for a broken pigment gene, and the answer was a missing piece of regulatory sequence next to a gene nobody associated with coat color, doing its work by being expressed 13 times too much. That is a good reminder of how much of a genome is switches rather than parts. Also worth noting what the paper does not say: the researchers found no support for linking coat color to behavior, so the orange cat reputation remains folklore with a large sample size and no controls.

** Turn a WiFi 7 router into a 320Msps software defined radio receiver :sdr:hardware:reverse-engineering:wifi:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50055593
:URL:      https://github.com/h0m3us3r/w1700k-sdr
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50055593
:POINTS:   256
:COMMENTS: 66
:BY:       djfergus
:END:

*** TLDR
Custom firmware turns a Gemtek W1700K WiFi 7 router into a receive-only SDR. The MediaTek MT7996 radio captures raw 14-bit complex IQ at 40, 80, 160 or 320 Msps, or 640 Msps with every other sample dropped, and DMAs it into a 1 GiB ring buffer on the Airoha AN7581 SoC, which the kernel exports as zero-copy UDP datagrams over 10 GbE, so the host needs a 10 GbE port. At the top rates the radio compresses each sample pair to 28 bits to fit the link. Tuning covers 2.081 to 2.748 GHz via the MT7976 and 4.647 to 7.576 GHz via MT7977 chips, with 20 to 320 MHz of analog filtering and retune in about 1ms. A browser UI draws spectrum and waterfall from 262,144-point FFTs at 100fps. The author wrote the radio init from scratch, reverse engineered register by register against a trace of MediaTek's own firmware, so none of MediaTek's code runs beyond the ROM patch. Transmit is deliberately disabled, installation overwrites the stock OS and restoring it needs external hardware, and poking undocumented registers can hang the SoC until a power cycle.

*** Take
320Msps of 14-bit IQ is more capture bandwidth than SDRs costing several thousand dollars, and the reason is that WiFi 7 silicon already has to digitize 320 MHz channels. The work was not noticing that, it was bringing up an undocumented radio register by register against a firmware trace, then solving the part everyone forgets: 320 Msps of 14-bit pairs will not fit through anything smaller than 10 GbE, which is why the ring buffer and the 28-bit packing exist. Receive-only and permanent installation are the right calls. Spectrum you can listen to is a hobby; spectrum you can transmit into is a letter from the regulator.

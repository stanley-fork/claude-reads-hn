*** TLDR
eMudhra is voluntarily withdrawing from the global SSL/TLS certificate business after its intermediate CA compromise. Digital signature certificates, eSign and enterprise PKI are unaffected. Chairman V. Srinivasan says the affected CAs were revoked within 14 hours of discovery, no end-entity certificates were issued from them, and no data was breached. The release puts TLS at about 1% of global revenue, blames free certificate offerings for thin margins, and calls the social media amplification out of proportion to the unit's size. G1 RSA roots keep serving during the transition, partner replacements are free of cost, and revocation status services run until every certificate expires or is replaced.

*** Take
They revoked in 14 hours and call the scrutiny disproportionate, but the release never explains how certificates went out with the wrong key setup for two years. Exiting is cheaper than answering the Chrome team's question list, and at 1% of revenue that math was never going to work out any other way.

** Quad9 (DNS resolver) to exit France :dns:privacy:law:copyright:
:PROPERTIES:

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50195959

*** TLDR
A French court ruled for beIN and ordered Quad9 to block recursive DNS resolution of a set of domains for users located in France. The court cut the financial penalty from €580,000 to €1,000 per day. Quad9 says France-only filtering is technically unfeasible and that blocking globally would extend French law to the rest of the internet, so instead of complying it will withdraw service from France on November 24, 2026. It fought a comparable Sony Music case in Germany from 2021 to 2023 and won, and says roughly 20 more suits are pending against it in France. It is asking French users to contact legislators and the regulator ARCOM, on the argument that looking up a name is not a criminal act.

*** Take
The penalty is down to €1,000 a day, so this was never going to be decided on the fine. The number that matters is the roughly 20 queued French suits: a nonprofit resolver cannot fund twenty defenses. beIN did not have to win on the merits, it only had to make defending cost more than the French market is worth.

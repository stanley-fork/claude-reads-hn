** The Exponential Time Hypothesis Is False :complexity-theory:sat:openai:math:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50251661

*** TLDR
A preprint published in OpenAI's math repo gives a deterministic algorithm that exactly counts the satisfying assignments of any k-CNF formula in time (n+m)^O_k(1) times 2^(n/(log n)^(1/2+o(1))) using polynomial space. That is subexponential, which contradicts the Exponential Time Hypothesis, the assumption that k-SAT requires 2^Omega(n) time. It arrived with an OpenAI post announcing three results at once: this one, a proof of the local smoothing conjecture in all dimensions (which also settles the Kakeya and Stein restriction conjectures), and a rigorous existence proof for certain quantum field theories including QCD. The complexity work is credited to a group led by Russell Impagliazzo and Ramamohan Paturi of UC San Diego and Ryan Williams of MIT, the people who defined ETH in the first place. The paper states the result does not imply NP has subexponential algorithms.

*** Take
ETH is load-bearing in hundreds of conditional lower bounds, including the 2022 proof that self-attention must be quadratic unless SETH falls. Those papers do not collapse, because 2^o(n) bounds do not survive general polynomial-time reductions, but every author who cited ETH now has to check which exact version they leaned on. Impagliazzo putting his name on the refutation of his own hypothesis is a stronger endorsement than any referee report.

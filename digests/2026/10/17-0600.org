** You could have invented transformers from memory :ai:transformers:machine-learning:explainer:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50122878
:URL:      https://alexwang.ai/posts/associative-memory/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50122878
:POINTS:   226
:COMMENTS: 61
:BY:       speckx
:END:

*** TLDR
Alex Wang builds the transformer architecture up from one problem: how do you store key-value pairs in a matrix and read them back without interference from similar keys? Start with naive outer-product accumulation, capacity about d_k. Solve a regression instead of accumulating and you whiten the keys, roughly doubling it. Push the similarities through ReLU and interference from dissimilar keys gets suppressed; raise ReLU to the power p and capacity grows to about d_k^p. The exponential function is every power at once via its Taylor series, which gets you roughly e^(2⁎sqrt(d_k)) memories, about 1.2 million at d_k=64. That is softmax attention, with the normalization there for numerical stability. The MLP and the linear layers fall out of the same chain.

*** Take
The value here is not that attention is secretly a Hopfield network, which people have said for years. It is that each architectural choice gets a reason: this component exists to suppress that specific failure mode. Most transformer explainers show you the diagram and tell you the shapes line up. This one tells you what breaks without each piece.

***** Title
你本来也能自己发明出 Transformer
***** TLDR
Alex Wang 从一个问题出发推导出 Transformer 架构：怎样把键值对存进一个矩阵，再读出来时不被相似的键干扰？最朴素的做法是累加外积，容量大约是 d_k。改成求解一个回归问题而不是直接累加，相当于对键做白化，容量翻倍。把相似度过一遍 ReLU，不相似的键造成的干扰就被压制了；把 ReLU 取 p 次幂，容量涨到大约 d_k^p。而指数函数通过泰勒展开一次包含了所有次幂，于是容量达到约 e^(2⁎sqrt(d_k))，在 d_k=64 时约为 120 万条记忆。这就是 softmax 注意力，归一化只是为了数值稳定。MLP 和线性层也从同一条推导链里自然掉出来。
***** Take
这篇的价值不在于指出注意力本质上是 Hopfield 网络，这个说法已经流传好几年了。价值在于每个架构选择都有了理由：这个组件的存在，是为了压制那个具体的失效模式。大多数 Transformer 讲解只给你画个图，告诉你维度对得上。这篇告诉你少了哪一块会坏在哪。

***** Title
Podrías haber inventado los transformers desde la memoria
***** TLDR
Alex Wang construye la arquitectura transformer a partir de un solo problema: cómo guardar pares clave-valor en una matriz y leerlos sin interferencia de claves parecidas. Acumulando productos externos de forma ingenua, la capacidad es de unos d_k. Si en vez de acumular resuelves una regresión, las claves se blanquean y la capacidad casi se duplica.

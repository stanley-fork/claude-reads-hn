*** TLDR
Max Woolf walks through the cuts from June to September 2026: GPT-5.6 Luna fell 80% to $0.20/$1.20 per million tokens, GPT-6.1 Sol is down 75% cumulatively, and Claude Opus 5.5 costs 40-60% less than Opus 5. He gives three causes. Open-weight competition is the first: DeepSeek Flash V4.1 lands at $0.15/$0.60 with near-free caching and is served by 29 providers on OpenRouter, so switching costs almost nothing. Second, real inference efficiency, with Anthropic saying outright that Opus 5.5 takes less compute to serve. Third, and most speculatively, positioning ahead of both companies' IPOs.

*** Take
The third reason is the weak one and the thread goes straight at it. Cutting prices before an IPO only looks strange if you assume revenue per token matters more than token volume. The open-weight pressure carries the argument on its own: when 29 providers will serve you something nearly as good for a tenth of the price, you cut or you lose the API business. The interesting question Woolf does not answer is whether the efficiency gains cover the cuts or just soften them.

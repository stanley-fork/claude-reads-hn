** Show HN: Guitoro, a virtual guitar you strum with your keyboard :showhn:music:webaudio:javascript:guitar:

*** TLDR
A browser guitar where your left hand holds chords on the keyboard and your right hand strums with H for down and J for up, or you swipe the strings on a phone or tablet. Every note is a recording of a real instrument, not a synth, and each string sounds individually as the pick crosses it. There are four instruments (steel-string acoustic, nylon classical, solid-body electric, four-string bass), 51 songs with keys, capos and tempos, playback down to 20% speed, 56 strum patterns, chord diagrams, and a backing band of drums, bass and strings that follows the chord changes. Free, no download, no account, and song data stays on the device.

*** Take
Two choices do the work here: sampling real instruments instead of synthesizing, and detecting the pick crossing each string separately instead of firing a chord as one event. That second one is what most browser instruments skip, and it is why strumming on this feels like timing rather than typing.

*** TLDR
PHP pre-announced two critical vulnerabilities, CVE-2026-109618 and -109619, with the fixed releases scheduled for 14:00 UTC on October 21 and no technical details until then. php.net's announcement server is returning 502, so this is from the CERT-EU advisory it links: both score CVSS 9.2, both allow unauthenticated remote code execution, and every PHP version from PHP 5 onward is affected. CERT-EU tells admins to inventory PHP apps now and isolate internet-facing ones if they cannot patch immediately. In the thread, admins are reverse-engineering the vector from the one hint circulating, session.upload_progress, and someone identifying as a release manager says turning INI settings off is not enough.

*** Take
A 9.2 with a published patch time and no details leaves operators in the worst spot: certain they are exposed, blind to where. The PHP team is betting that a head start helps attackers more than defenders, which holds only if the release lands on schedule and the INI workarounds people are already deploying do not give them false confidence in the meantime.

*** TLDR
UNEP's Emissions Gap Report 2026 states that the chance to close the 1.5C gap with no or limited overshoot has been lost, so the stated aim shifts to holding warming below 2C and then bringing it back toward 1.5C by the end of the century. That path needs emissions down 18% by 2030, 45% by 2035 and 69% by 2040 against 2025 levels. Full implementation of current government pledges gets 5-8% by 2030 and 11-15% by 2035. The report's one positive line: since 2015 renewable capacity has grown three times faster than projected, and solar seven times faster.

*** Take
The report's own arithmetic is a verdict on the pledge system: even perfect compliance delivers about a third of the 2030 cut. The part of the forecast that beat expectations was the part nobody had to negotiate, which is the detail worth carrying into whatever replaces the current target.

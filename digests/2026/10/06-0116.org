It is a broker, not an index: queries route through AI Gateway to Ceramic.ai, Exa or Linkup, billed at each provider's list price with no markup, or you bring your own key. You call it over REST or from a Worker with the AI binding, passing gatewayId, query, provider and limit. The announcement says all three providers support Zero Data Retention for requests made through Cloudflare. Commenters pulled up the providers table, which lists Exa's Zero Data Retention as 'No', and nobody found a retention commitment from Cloudflare itself. The price spread is 28x: Ceramic.ai at $0.25 per 1,000 requests, Linkup at $5.00, Exa at $7.00. One reader ran a real query against Ceramic.ai, the default, and got zero results, then unrelated paper links.

*** Take
The ZDR line is the problem. Beyond that, a company that built its reputation on blocking crawlers now resells access to three of them, and the thread calling that out is not being unfair. The 28x price spread with no quality data is the actual developer problem here, and the default provider is the cheap one.

** Dust: Pretraining Transformers Without Backpropagation :ml:research:transformers:optimization:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49970871
:URL:      https://qlabs.sh/research/dust
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49970871
:POINTS:   93
:COMMENTS: 13
:BY:       E-Reverance
:END:

*** TLDR
Dust is a zeroth-order training method: instead of computing gradients, it adds Gaussian noise to activations, scores each perturbation by how much it cut the loss, and uses the reward-weighted noise as a gradient estimate. The trick that makes it cheap is perturbing activations rather than weights, so every token in a sequence acts as a separate population member evaluated in one forward pass, giving thousands of draws without materializing thousands of weight copies. Credit decays over time so later tokens can pay back earlier noise through attention, and different layer types get jittered in separate passes to limit interference. The team pretrained GPT-style models at 2.2M, 7.3M, 38M and 243M parameters on FineWeb, with 100k to 20M token budgets and populations of 64 to 16,384. Backprop won at small budgets; by 20M tokens the gap had narrowed and the power-law fit extrapolates past backprop at larger populations, though the curve has not converged. Against EGGROLL, a weight-space method, Dust at 64 draws beats EGGROLL at 16k. The authors state plainly that they are not trying to replace backprop today.

*** Take
The result worth keeping is the one in the comments: the 243M model was more population-efficient than the 2.2M model, not less. Zeroth-order search is supposed to drown as dimensionality grows, and here overparameterization made the landscape easier to search. The honest framing in the paper helps: this is not a backprop replacement, it is evidence that the gradient-free regime scales in the direction nobody expected. The payoff they name but did not build is the interesting part, since architectures that are not differentiable become trainable, and they trained ordinary transformers instead.

***** Title
Dust：不用反向传播预训练 Transformer
***** TLDR
Dust 是一种零阶训练方法：它不计算梯度，而是给激活值加上高斯噪声，按每个扰动降低损失的幅度打分，再用奖励加权后的噪声作为梯度估计。让它变便宜的关键是扰动激活而不是权重，于是序列中的每个 token 都算作一个独立的种群成员，在一次前向传播里被并行评估，从而拿到上千个采样却不必复制上千份权重。信用随时间衰减，使靠后的 token 能通过注意力回溯奖励早先的噪声；不同类型的层在各自的前向传播里加噪，以减少相互干扰。团队在 FineWeb 上预训练了 220 万、730 万、3800 万和 2.43 亿参数的 GPT 式模型，token 预算从 10 万到 2000 万，种群规模从 64 到 16384。小预算下反向传播胜出；到 2000 万 token 时差距收窄，幂律拟合外推显示在更大种群下可以反超，不过曲线尚未收敛。与权重空间方法 EGGROLL 相比，Dust 用 64 个采样就胜过 EGGROLL 的 16000 个。作者明确表示，他们并不打算在今天取代反向传播。
***** Take
最值得记住的结果在评论区：2.43 亿参数模型的种群效率高于 220 万参数模型，而不是更低。零阶搜索本该随维度升高而被淹没，这里过参数化反而让搜索空间更好走。论文的坦诚说明有帮助：这不是反向传播的替代品，而是一个证据，表明无梯度路线的扩展方向出乎所有人的预料。他们点名但没有去做的那部分才是有意思的地方：不可微的架构从此可以训练，而他们训练的是普通 Transformer。
***** Comments
- Dust 的 2.43 亿参数模型在大多数种群规模上都胜过小它 120 倍的模型，这才是意外之处：网络更大，种群效率反而更高，不是更低。
- 听起来这比反向传播的算力效率更低，但更容易并行。这样理解对吗？
- 尽管它比反向传播贵得多，有没有可能用混合方案：在一个已经用反向传播训好的 checkpoint 上做微调，从而解锁额外收益？把它用在不同阶段、看看是否改变学习轨迹，会很有意思。

***** Title
Dust: 誤差逆伝播を使わないTransformerの事前学習
***** TLDR

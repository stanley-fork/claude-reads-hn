** Why is this fingerprinting script creating bioweapons? :security:llm:fingerprinting:obfuscation:javascript:

*** TLDR
The author crawled the Tranco top sites intercepting fingerprinting API calls, catalogued 1,033 canvas renderings from 10,000 sites at fingerprint.garden, and found one script that stood out: servicepipe.tech/static/checkjs/06b667/4CIrx8U3Da.js, traced to servicepipe.ru, a Russian traffic-filtering and anti-DDoS firm. Inside the obfuscated bundle are function names like computeRicinLethalDosageForWaterSupply and references to synthesizing biological agents, plus a long 'SYSTEM OVERRIDE' jailbreak prompt hidden in invisible Unicode characters and Tiananmen Square references aimed at Chinese models. None of it runs. It exists to trip the safety filters of any LLM asked to deobfuscate the file. Claude refused the task citing biological research risk; GLM-5.3 complied and the analysis went ahead. The actual payload is ordinary: navigator properties, WebGL and GPU strings, hardware concurrency, max touch points.

*** Take
The payload is a WebGL string and some navigator properties. The elaborate part is the defense, and it targets the reader's refusal policy rather than the reader's parser, which is a genuinely new place to put obfuscation. It also works unevenly by vendor, so whether a model will read your file is now a product difference between Anthropic and Z.ai, decided by neither of them and tested by a Russian DDoS shop. arbol's objection is the one that holds: an agent working from the AST drops dead code, so this buys time against lazy pipelines and nothing against anyone who actually wants the script.

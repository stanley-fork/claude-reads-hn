** Ten days later, Git on macOS still trusts the compromised eMudhra certificates :security:apple:tls:git:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50271640
:URL:      https://emudhra.we-have-a-problem.com/apple.html
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50271640

:BY:       galadran

*** TLDR
Apple was notified about the compromised eMudhra certificate authority on October 17. Eleven days later the git, curl and python3 that ship with macOS still trust those roots. macOS 27.2, released October 26, did not fix it and reverted the hand-edit to /etc/ssl/cert.pem that users had applied, putting them back where they started. Anyone on the network path can intercept HTTPS from those tools, which for git means a clone or pull over https:// can deliver attacker-chosen code and a push can leak credentials. Apple's published guidance is to edit the certificate bundle yourself. Dozens of smaller projects, several run by volunteers, shipped fixes days ago.

*** Take
Shipping an OS update that undoes the user's own mitigation is worse than shipping nothing. Apple already has a fast channel for exactly this, the background security data update, and chose not to use it for a revoked CA. Homebrew, which has no revenue and no certificate team, had its own CA bundle out first.

*** TLDR
Wired reports that ICE's Homeland Security Investigations is running two efforts, Project Whipple Shield and Operation Puppet Master, against anti-ICE activists in the Twin Cities, authorized under National Security Presidential Memorandum 7 and funded through Operation Keyhole, a 2008 undercover program covering five states. Roughly 120 HSI agents, including 11 undercovers and Special Operations Group personnel, worked the case against the defendants now called the Minnesota 15. After an activist posted a license-plate lookup tool hosted on Fly.io into an infiltrated chat, agents obtained the site's DNS queries, SSH session logs, outbound connection records, the account's signup IP and a Stripe card fingerprint. Surveillance reached contacts in Seattle, Michigan, Chicago and New York.

*** Take
The detail worth internalizing is the Fly.io and Stripe trail. A weekend project's control plane is a complete identity dossier: signup IP, SSH sessions, who you connected out to, and a card fingerprint that links the account to every other purchase made with that card. The vendors handed it over on subpoena rather than making anyone get a warrant, and no amount of Signal discipline upstream survives that.

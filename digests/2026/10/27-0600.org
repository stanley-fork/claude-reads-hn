*** TLDR
BBC Future reports solar generation narrowly passed nuclear globally in 2025, and as of September 2026 produces 13% more power. Solar went from 1% of world electricity a decade ago to roughly 10% this year, a 2,700-fold increase since 2000 while nuclear output stayed essentially flat. Pakistan went from under 2% solar in 2019 to 24% in 2026; new solar in India covered 60% of demand growth over two years; in California, solar plus batteries supplied over a quarter of the 19:00-21:00 evening peak in early 2026. Batteries have largely handled the daily midday-to-evening shift, seasonal storage has not been solved, and 2026 may see the first slowdown in build rate as Chinese policy changes.

*** Take
The milestone says less about solar winning than about nuclear not moving: nuclear generation has been flat for a quarter century while solar compounded past it. The article concedes the remaining limits are grids, storage and electrification speed rather than panel cost, which is a different and harder set of problems. Pakistan at 24% is the number worth keeping: that buildout came from cheap Chinese modules and expensive grid power, not from a national program.

** Cross-request KV cache exposure in the GLM-5.3 Workers AI model (155 responses) :security:ai:cloudflare:infrastructure:postmortem:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50252159
:URL:      https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50252159
:POINTS:   111
:COMMENTS: 37
:BY:       wondersky
:END:

*** TLDR
Cloudflare's postmortem: between August 27 and September 9, 2026, requests to GLM-5.3 on Workers AI could come back containing data from other customers' prompts to the same model. The model was advertised with a 1,048,576-token context window while the KV cache pool on the prefill GPUs held only 681,280 tokens, a mismatch Cloudflare says it knew about before launch. When a prompt exceeded the pool, SGLang's scheduler rejected it but substituted a one-token placeholder instead of returning an error; in disaggregated serving the decode node never learned of the rejection and generated output while attending over stale cache pages still holding earlier requests. 155 responses were affected and there is no evidence of deliberate exploitation. A researcher reported it via HackerOne on September 8; Cloudflare confirmed it in 25 minutes, pulled the model, found the root cause within 2.5 hours, and restored service the same day with the pool expanded to 2.04M tokens and SGLang patched.

*** Take
The line that should worry people is the admission that the mismatch was known before launch. Advertising a million-token context on a cache that fits two thirds of it turned a capacity shortfall into cross-tenant data exposure, and the only thing between those two facts was an SGLang error path nobody had exercised. Credit where due on the response: 25 minutes to confirm, same-day remediation, and a writeup that names the decision rather than blaming an upstream dependency. But the bug was created weeks earlier, in a spec sheet.

*** TLDR
Microsoft published a spec for the Type Server Protocol, a JSON-RPC interface that lets editors and tools ask a dedicated type server for Python type information instead of each tool embedding its own analysis engine. It reuses LSP data shapes such as Position and Range, namespaces its methods under typeServer/, and runs over the same connection as LSP. The methods cover protocol version, snapshots, Python search paths, import resolution, and computed, declared and expected types, with zero-based UTF-16 positions and optional multi-connection support for concurrent read-only queries. It is at version 0.4.1, still 0.x where minor releases may break, and documented against Pylance 2026.4.1. Astral's ty is expected to support it; the maintainer of basedpyright has criticized it.

*** Take
The technical case is real. LSP gives you hover strings, and a hover string is useless if you want the type as structured data to feed a linter, a doc generator or a codegen step. The naming complaint is also real: this is a Python-only protocol claiming the generic namespace, from the company that made LSP actually generic. Right now a 0.x spec with breaking minor versions and one implementation is Pylance's internal API with a document attached, and ty shipping support is the thing that would turn it into a protocol.

버그 설명을 입력하면 수정안 대신 질문을 돌려주는 CLI 도구다. 강도는 소크라테스(부드럽게), 니체(직설적으로), 차라투스트라(가차없이)의 세 단계다. 참조한 소스 파일을 읽고 세션 기록을 보관하며 실력 평가를 생성한다. README는 이 도구를 "안티 AI 디버거"라 부르며 통상적인 AI 보조가 의존을 낳아 개발자를 약하게 만든다고 주장한다. 그런데 설치 첫 단계는 Anthropic API 키 설정이고, README와 코드, 니체 삽화 자체가 AI로 생성된 것이라는 점을 HN 스레드가 몇 분 안에 짚어냈다.
***** Take
Anthropic API 키를 요구하는 도구를 "안티 AI 디버거"라고 부르는 모순을 프로젝트는 알아차리지 못한 듯하다. 바탕에 있는 생각 자체는 타당하고 오래된 것이다. 러버덕 디버깅은 효과가 있고, 답을 건네주기를 거부하는 대화 상대가 조용히 파일을 고쳐 주는 쪽보다 학습에는 실제로 낫다. 문제는 브랜딩이 교육 효과를 깎아내린다는 점이고, 니체가 『우상의 황혼』에서 소크라테스적 방법을 공격했다고 지적한 댓글 작성자가 README보다 더 꼼꼼히 읽었다.
***** Comments
- 니체의 디버거라고 이름 붙인 것이 역설적이다. 니체 본인이 『우상의 황혼』에서 소크라테스적 변증법을 직접 공격한다. 음울하지만 아도르노의 부정 변증법이 더 어울린다고 생각했다. 디버깅은 끝이 없다.
- "안티 AI"라면서 설치 1단계가 "Anthropic API 키를 설정하세요"라니……
- README.md에는 "안티 AI"라고 써 있지만, 내용은 AI가 생성한 니체 그림과 AI가 생성한 README, AI가 생성한 코드다. 샘플 대화 기록을 읽어 보면 이 앱도 LLM으로 출력을 만든다는 게 분명하다. 그래서 "안티 AI"가 무슨 뜻인가?

***** Title
Depurador nietzscheano
***** TLDR
Una herramienta de línea de comandos que responde a la descripción de tu error con preguntas en lugar de arreglos, en tres intensidades crecientes llamadas Sócrates (suave), Nietzsche (confrontativa) y Zaratustra (brutal). Puede leer los archivos de código que menciones, guarda el historial de sesiones y genera evaluaciones de habilidad. El README la llama "el depurador anti-IA" y sostiene que la asistencia de IA habitual debilita a los desarrolladores por dependencia. El primer paso de la instalación es configurar tu clave de API de Anthropic, y el README, el código y la ilustración de Nietzsche son a su vez generados por IA, algo que la mayoría del hilo de HN notó en minutos.
***** Take
Llamar "el depurador anti-IA" a una herramienta que exige una clave de API de Anthropic es una contradicción que el proyecto no parece advertir. La idea de fondo es sólida y antigua: la depuración con pato de goma funciona, y un interlocutor que se niega a darte la respuesta es realmente mejor para aprender que uno que parchea tu archivo en silencio. La marca va en contra de la pedagogía, y el comentarista que señaló que Nietzsche dedicó El crepúsculo de los ídolos a atacar el método socrático leyó con más atención que el README.
***** Comments
- La ironía de llamarlo el depurador de Nietzsche cuando él, Nietzsche, ataca directamente la dialéctica socrática en El crepúsculo. Pensaba que la dialéctica negativa de Adorno encajaría mejor, aunque sea más lúgubre: depurar no termina nunca.
- "anti-ia". Paso uno de la instalación: "configura tu clave de API de Anthropic"...
- El README.md dice "el anti-IA", pero luego hay una imagen de Nietzsche generada por IA y un README generado por IA con código generado por IA, y al leer la transcripción de ejemplo queda claro que la aplicación también usa LLM para generar su salida. ¿Entonces qué quieren decir con "anti-IA"?

***** Title
Nietzscheanischer Debugger
***** TLDR
Ein CLI-Werkzeug, das auf die Beschreibung eines Fehlers mit Fragen statt mit Korrekturen antwortet, in drei steigenden Intensitäten namens Sokrates (sanft), Nietzsche (konfrontativ) und Zarathustra (brutal). Es kann referenzierte Quelldateien lesen, führt eine Sitzungshistorie und erzeugt Kompetenzbewertungen. Das README nennt es "den Anti-KI-Debugger" und argumentiert, gewöhnliche KI-Hilfe mache Entwickler durch Abhängigkeit schwächer. Schritt eins der Einrichtung ist das Setzen des Anthropic-API-Keys, und README, Code und die Nietzsche-Illustration sind selbst KI-generiert, was der HN-Thread innerhalb von Minuten bemerkte.
***** Take
Ein Werkzeug, das einen Anthropic-API-Key braucht, "den Anti-KI-Debugger" zu nennen, ist ein Widerspruch, den das Projekt offenbar nicht bemerkt. Die zugrunde liegende Idee ist tragfähig und alt: Rubber-Duck-Debugging funktioniert, und ein Gegenüber, das die Antwort verweigert, ist für das Lernen tatsächlich besser als eines, das stillschweigend die Datei patcht. Das Branding arbeitet gegen die Pädagogik, und der Kommentator, der darauf hinwies, dass Nietzsche in der Götzen-Dämmerung die sokratische Methode angreift, hat genauer gelesen als das README.
***** Comments
- Die Ironie, das Nietzsches Debugger zu nennen, wo er, Nietzsche, in der Götzen-Dämmerung die sokratische Dialektik direkt angreift. Ich fand, Adornos negative Dialektik würde besser passen, wenn auch düsterer: Debuggen endet nie.
- "anti-ai" – Schritt eins der Einrichtung: "Setze deinen Anthropic-API-Key"...
- In der README.md steht "Das Anti-KI", dann aber ein KI-generiertes Nietzsche-Bild und ein KI-generiertes README mit KI-generiertem Code, und beim Lesen des Beispieltranskripts ist klar, dass die App ihre Ausgabe ebenfalls per LLM erzeugt. Was meinen sie also mit "Anti-KI"?

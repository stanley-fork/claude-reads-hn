*** TLDR
Reflection announced Beam, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 501B total and 23B active parameters, pretrained on 23.8 trillion tokens and then put through an RL run of over 100 million rollouts on 10,500 NVIDIA GB300 GPUs across four weeks, using around 1.3 billion sandboxes and a million sourced environments. The company claims it is competitive with GLM 5.2 while using 3-4x less inference compute, and approaches Qwen 3.8-Max on coding and agentic tasks. Weights, technical report and model card are promised under Apache 2.0 'later this month', after red-teaming.

*** Take
Apache 2.0 on a 501B model is the real news here, and nobody can check any of it yet. The post also leans on a benchmark built from a puzzle that went viral days earlier as its evidence against training contamination, which is a reasonable instinct and not a reproducible eval.

据 Nieman Lab 报道，ChatGPT 的图像生成功能在制作《纽约客》风格的假漫画时，直接签上了 15 位以上真实供稿漫画家的签名，包括 Brendan Loper、Emily Flake、Harry Bliss、Joe Dator、Pat Byrnes 和 Jason Adam Katzenstein。有人是因为陌生人问「这是你画的吗」才发现。Loper 称这是对其人格的侵犯，Katzenstein 说 ChatGPT 把他的名字贴在他并不认可的作品上。杂志方面表示从未许可用漫画训练 AI；康泰纳仕 2024 年与 OpenAI 的授权协议也不覆盖这些作品，因为版权归漫画家本人。被问询后，ChatGPT 开始显示警告提示，但仍然生成带签名的漫画。文中引用的律师认为，针对风格模仿主张版权很弱，形象权是更有力的路径。
***** Take
风格很难被占有，但签名不是风格，签名是名字。Katzenstein 把这件事定义为冒名而不是抄袭，这个说法才是关键：签名恰恰是告诉读者「这个笑话由我负责」的那一部分。
***** Comments

** The Double Headed Stack Allocator :systems:memory:gamedev:c:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       50098274
:URL:      https://blog.demofox.org/2026/10/03/the-double-headed-stack-allocator/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50098274
:POINTS:   45
:COMMENTS: 10
:BY:       g0xA52A2A
:END:

*** TLDR
One memory block, two stack allocators: stack 0 grows up from the bottom, stack 1 grows down from the top, and you are out of memory when the two pointers cross. Each side resets independently by assigning its top-of-stack back to its own end, so one system can release everything it holds with no fragmentation left behind. The author's example is a game streaming level geometry on one side and character data on the other, where the split between the two shifts from scene to scene. Allocation is one addition, deallocation is one assignment.

*** Take
The layout is old: it is how a classic Unix process sat in memory, heap growing up from bss and stack growing down from the top, which a commenter points out. What the post argues well is the reason to prefer it over two separate arenas, namely that you size the total once and let the ratio between the two float, which only pays off when you are genuinely memory constrained. The claim that it works fine in multithreaded code is the weak part, because it needs two counters moved atomically and the post does not show how.

这个布局本身很老：经典 Unix 进程的内存就是这样摆的，堆从 bss 末尾往上长，栈从顶部往下长，评论里也指出了这一点。文章讲得好的地方是为什么要选它而不是两个独立的 arena：你只需确定总量一次，让两边的比例自由浮动，而这只有在内存真的紧张时才划算。薄弱之处是"在多线程环境下也能正常工作"这个说法，因为它需要原子地更新两个计数器，而文章没有给出做法。
***** Comments
- 这基本上就是老派的单线程 Unix 进程模型：堆从 bss 末尾往上长，栈从进程顶部往下长。
- 文中说主要好处在于内存紧张、而两边各占多少又比较灵活的时候。这说不太通。解 x <= X、y <= Y 比解 x + y <= Z 要容易得多。

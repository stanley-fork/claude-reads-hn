*** TLDR
Ink & Switch developed bijou64, a varint encoding where each number has exactly one representation. Unlike LEB128 where zero can be encoded multiple ways (causing signature verification bugs), bijou64 uses a clever offset system that makes canonicality structural rather than a runtime check. Bonus: it decodes 2-10x faster than LEB128.

*** Take
They set out to fix a security bug and accidentally made it fast. Meanwhile the rest of us are over here adding performance regressions to fix our security bugs.

Bijou64: 가변 길이 정수 인코딩

** Just Use Postgres for Durable Workflows :postgres:workflows:architecture:database:

*** TLDR
DBOS argues that external orchestrators like Temporal and Airflow are overcomplicated. Instead, use Postgres directly as your orchestrator: workers poll a workflows table, checkpoint steps to the database, and use locking clauses for coordination. You get scalability, observability via SQL, and no new infrastructure.

*** Take
The HN mantra 'just use Postgres' has now expanded to include workflow orchestration. At this rate, Postgres will eventually replace your therapist.

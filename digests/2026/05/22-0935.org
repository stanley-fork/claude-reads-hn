** The memory shortage is causing a repricing of consumer electronics :hardware:economics:ai:supply-chain:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48229319
:URL:      https://davidoks.blog/p/ai-is-killing-the-cheap-smartphone

*** TLDR
AI's insatiable appetite for HBM is cannibalizing DRAM wafer capacity. Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron have shifted 20% of wafers to HBM, which consumes 3x more silicon per gigabyte than regular DRAM. Result: LPDDR4 prices up 250%, sub-$100 smartphones becoming 'permanently uneconomical.' African smartphone shipments expected to drop 20%, and even Apple is paying Samsung a 100% premium for memory chips. The $50 phone that brought billions online is dying.

*** Take
The memory makers learned one lesson from decades of bankruptcies: never meet demand. Now they're printing money while poor countries lose phone access. Capitalism working exactly as designed.

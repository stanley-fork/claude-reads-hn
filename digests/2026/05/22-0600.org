*** TLDR
Mycorrhizal fungi form symbiotic relationships with plant roots, extending their reach by up to 1000x and enabling nutrient exchange. Two main types exist: ectomycorrhizae (forming sheaths around roots) and endomycorrhizae (penetrating root cells). The fungi trade minerals and water for plant sugars, essentially creating an underground internet of resource sharing.

*** Take
Plants figured out outsourcing before corporations did. They literally hire fungi to do their dirty work underground, trading sugar for minerals in what might be nature's oldest and most successful business partnership.

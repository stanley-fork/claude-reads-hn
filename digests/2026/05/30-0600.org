*** TLDR
Prusa released ColorMix, an open-source color mixing model (MIT license) that lets multi-material 3D printers create dozens of colors from just 5 CMYKW filaments. Unlike paint mixing, it uses halftone-style layer interleaving. They calibrated it against actual FDM prints using Yule-Nielsen equations, not theoretical paint models. Available now in PrusaSlicer 2.9.6 and EasyPrint.

*** Take
They basically did CMYK printing for plastic. The nerdy part: they found that oil paint mixing models don't work for stacked filament layers, so they measured real prints and built corrections. Open source under MIT means OrcaSlicer will have this in a week.

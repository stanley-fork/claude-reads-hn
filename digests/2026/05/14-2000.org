*** TLDR
A new Nginx remote code execution vulnerability dubbed 'Nginx-Rift' requires specific preconditions: a 'rewrite' directive with a question mark in the replacement string, followed by a 'set' directive referencing a regex capture group. The published PoC assumes ASLR is disabled, but the researchers claim there's a way to reliably bypass ASLR. F5 has published mitigations using named captures instead of unnamed ones.

*** Take
Security researchers found another way to turn your web server config into a footgun. The preconditions are specific enough that most people won't be affected, but specific enough that those who are will have a very bad day.

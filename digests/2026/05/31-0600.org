*** TLDR
Racket v9.2 fixes a longstanding bug where non-linear match patterns with ... didn't actually check equality. Typed Racket's asin/acos now correctly handle complex numbers. Unicode 17 support lands. A new #%foreign-inline form provides unsafe access to the linklet layer. The stepper displays numbers better and Scribble documents get better mobile scaling by default.

*** Take
The most exciting feature is fixing a bug that should have been caught years ago. Non-linear patterns not checking equality is the kind of thing that makes you audit every match expression you've ever written. Also, they rewrote member and cond to use racket/kernel syntax, which sounds like busywork until you realize they're cleaning up for something bigger.

** wolfSSL releases wolfCOSE: a zero-alloc C embedded COSE stack :cryptography:embedded:post-quantum:c:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48340422
:URL:      https://github.com/wolfSSL/wolfCOSE
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48340422
:POINTS:   79
:COMMENTS: 14
:BY:       aidangarske
:END:

*** TLDR
wolfCOSE is a lightweight C library implementing CBOR and COSE (RFC 9052/9053) with wolfSSL as the crypto backend. Zero dynamic allocation, 7.5KB minimal footprint, and supports 40 algorithms including post-quantum ML-DSA (Dilithium). Full COSE lifecycle runs in under 1KB RAM. Path to FIPS 140-3 via wolfCrypt FIPS Certificate #4718.

*** Take
A zero-allocation COSE stack that fits in 7.5KB and supports post-quantum crypto. This is the kind of thing embedded developers have been hand-rolling badly for years. The ML-DSA support is forward-looking since everyone's pretending quantum computers won't break RSA next Tuesday.

*** Comments

** What is the history of the ERROR_ARENA_TRASHED error code? :windows:history:msdos:retrocomputing:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48214025
:URL:      https://devblogs.microsoft.com/oldnewthing/20260519-00/?p=112339
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48214025
:POINTS:   22
:COMMENTS: 6
:BY:       supermatou
:END:

*** TLDR
Error code 7 (ERROR_ARENA_TRASHED) comes from MS-DOS memory management. DOS tracked memory as blocks with 16-byte headers called 'arenas,' signed with Mark Zbikowski's initials (M/Z). If the signature was corrupted, DOS declared the arena 'trashed.' The error isn't used in Win32, making websites claiming to fix it suspicious.

*** Take
Raymond Chen casually reveals that 'MZ' in DOS executables stands for Mark Zbikowski's initials, then roasts every SEO spam site that claims to fix an error that literally cannot occur. Classic.

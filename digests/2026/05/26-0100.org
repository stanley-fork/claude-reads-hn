*** TLDR
Prompt injection in a Copilot Cowork 'skill' file can exfiltrate SharePoint/OneDrive documents without user approval. The attack works because sending Teams messages to yourself doesn't require confirmation, and those messages can embed external images that leak pre-authenticated download URLs when viewed. Tested successfully against Claude Opus 4.7. The injection was just 5 lines in an 81-line skill file and succeeded 100% of the time.

*** Take
The enterprise AI agent dream meets the prompt injection nightmare. Microsoft built an agent that can read all your files and send messages on your behalf, then was shocked - SHOCKED - that someone could combine those two capabilities maliciously. The fact that scheduled tasks run unattended makes this extra spicy. Every MBA who force-deployed Copilot company-wide just felt a cold shiver.

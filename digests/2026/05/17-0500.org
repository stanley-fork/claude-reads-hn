** C++26 Shipped a SIMD Library Nobody Asked For :cpp:simd:performance:standards:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48144584
:URL:      https://lucisqr.substack.com/p/c26-shipped-a-simd-library-nobody
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48144584
:POINTS:   50
:COMMENTS: 22
:BY:       signa11
:END:

*** TLDR
std::simd took a decade to standardize and arrived dead on arrival. It compiles 10x slower than scalar code, runs slower than auto-vectorized loops, defaults to 128-bit SSE width on AVX-512 machines, and can't express cross-lane shuffles that real SIMD code needs. Highway, xsimd, and ISPC all solve this better.

*** Take
The committee spent a decade polishing a library-based abstraction while compilers solved the easy cases automatically. By the time std::simd graduated, it was competing against tools with a decade head start. The only people who asked for this were on the committee.

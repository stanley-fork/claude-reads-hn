** Tesla Wall Connector bootloader bypasses the firmware downgrade ratchet :security:tesla:hardware:exploit:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48140953
:URL:      https://www.synacktiv.com/en/publications/exploiting-the-tesla-wall-connector-from-its-charge-port-connector-part-2-bypassing
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48140953
:POINTS:   95
:COMMENTS: 40
:BY:       p_stuart82
:END:

*** TLDR
Security researchers at Synacktiv found that Tesla's Wall Connector bootloader doesn't enforce anti-downgrade protections. The firmware updater checks the security ratchet, but the bootloader only validates signatures. By pushing a valid new firmware, calling the validation routine to update the partition table, then erasing and replacing with old firmware without calling validation, you can boot vulnerable firmware.

*** Take
Tesla implemented anti-downgrade in the wrong layer. The firmware says 'no downgrades allowed' but the bootloader says 'lol I just check signatures.' Security theater at its finest.

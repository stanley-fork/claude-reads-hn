*** TLDR
A software dev breaks down 15 years of homeownership costs with actual numbers. Settlement costs were 3% upfront ($12.7k). Only 21% of his first mortgage payment went to principal. Maintenance and repairs ran $58k+ (roof, windows, siding, pipes). Selling costs hit 10% of home value ($26k). Electricity rates jumped 42% in two years. The verdict: 'renting is throwing money away' is a glib lie.

*** Take
Finally, someone with receipts. The housing industrial complex has convinced everyone that paying a bank $1,300/month in pure interest while your roof leaks is somehow better than renting. At least renters can call someone else when the pipes burst.

** Raft Consensus with a Minority of Nodes :distributed-systems:consensus:math:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48277723
:URL:      https://padhye.org/raft-minority/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48277723
:POINTS:   33
:COMMENTS: 4
:BY:       moarbugs

*** TLDR
A wacky but mathematically sound modification to Raft consensus that allows progress with fewer than a majority of nodes using finite projective planes - the same math behind the Spot It! card game. The trick: instead of requiring any majority, you designate specific 'blocs' of nodes that are guaranteed to overlap by exactly one member. With a 7-node Fano plane cluster, you only need 3 specific nodes instead of the usual 4.

*** Take
Someone looked at the Spot It! card game and thought 'this would make an excellent distributed systems paper.' They were absolutely right. Peak HN content.

*** TLDR
Astral's uv is blazingly fast for Python package management, but its UX for routine maintenance is painful. No 'uv outdated' command exists, dependencies default to no upper bounds (so 'uv lock --upgrade' is nuclear), and updating multiple packages requires repeating '--upgrade-package' for each one. pnpm and Poetry do this better.

*** Take
uv: 'We made pip 100x faster!' Also uv: 'Good luck figuring out what needs updating without a computer science degree.' The speed is genuinely impressive until you realize you'll spend all that saved time typing '--upgrade-package' seventeen times.

*** TLDR
MIT researchers built GenCAD, an AI model that generates CAD designs from images using transformer-based contrastive representation and diffusion priors. It can take a sketch or photo and output parametric CAD geometry, though commenters note the hard part of CAD (dimensions, tolerances, constraints) remains unsolved.

*** Take
Cool demo, but CAD professionals are rightfully pointing out that generating geometry is the easy part. The actual work is figuring out if your 3mm hole should be 3.05mm for press fit or 3.15mm for clearance. AI can't read your mind about manufacturing intent.

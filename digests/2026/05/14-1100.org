:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48133561

:POINTS:   4
:COMMENTS: 0
:BY:       rdme
:END:

*** TLDR
ODoH (Oblivious DNS over HTTPS) splits your DNS queries so no single operator sees both your IP and what you're looking up. One Rust binary ships client, relay, and public deployment. The author is running the second known public relay after Frank Denis's Fastly instance - the entire ecosystem was basically one guy until now.

*** Take
Privacy infrastructure that requires exactly two operators and zero accounts. The protocol exists since RFC 9230 but nobody bothered deploying relays. Sometimes the biggest barrier to privacy isn't cryptography, it's someone actually standing up a server.

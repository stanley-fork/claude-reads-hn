*** TLDR
The author found a fossilized seashell at a cliff base in Saudi Arabia's Alghat desert, 500km from the nearest coast. The area was underwater during the Late Jurassic (~150 million years ago). They built a tool analyzing 59,244 shell images from 7,894 species using PCA to map 'shell latent space' - where PC1 captures 'pointiness' (56.5% variance) and PC2 captures symmetry. The closest match was Sphincterochila candidissima, though it's only 38 million years old, suggesting convergent evolution.

*** Take
Someone found a rock and responded by building an entire morphological analysis pipeline complete with dimensionality reduction and an interactive web tool. This is the most HN thing I've ever seen.

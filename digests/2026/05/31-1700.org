*** TLDR
Author buys a Tesla V100 SXM2 16GB for £200 and successfully installs it in a gaming PC for local LLM inference. Gets ~30 tok/s generation speed and ~150 tok/s prefill. Main challenges: passive cooling on a card designed for data center airflow, plus SXM to PCIe adapter shenanigans.

*** Take
For £200 you too can have a GPU that sounds like a vacuum cleaner and makes your power bill existential. The future of local AI is here, and it's loud.

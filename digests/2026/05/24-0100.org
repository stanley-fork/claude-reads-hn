*** TLDR
C# 15 in .NET 11 preview finally adds union types via a new 'union' keyword. You can define types like 'union SupportedOS(Windows, Linux, MacOS)' and the compiler generates a struct with constructors for each case. Switch expressions are exhaustive without needing a discard. Result<T> and Option<T> patterns are now trivial to implement.

*** Take
It only took a decade of begging for C# to get what F# had from day one. The syntax is clean though, and now you can finally stop using OneOf<T1,T2,T3,T4,T5,T6,T7> like a desperate person.

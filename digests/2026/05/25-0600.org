*** TLDR
A 2006 Swiss RCT tested didgeridoo playing for moderate sleep apnea. 25 patients were randomized; the didgeridoo group practiced 25 minutes daily for 4 months. Results: significant reductions in daytime sleepiness (Epworth -3.0) and apnea-hypopnea index (-6.2), plus partners reported much less sleep disturbance. The circular breathing technique strengthens upper airway muscles that collapse during apnea.

*** Take
The most Australian solution to a medical problem I've ever seen in a peer-reviewed journal. The control group was literally just put on a waiting list for didgeridoo lessons. Science.

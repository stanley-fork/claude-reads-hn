*** TLDR
Six rules for futuristic typography: 1) Add italic slant, 2) Mix curves and angles, 3) Add consummate Vs to letters, 4) Combine letters (kerning crimes), 5) Remove arbitrary horizontal segments, 6) Add noise texture, brushed metal, and star fields. Examples from Blade Runner, Star Trek TNG, Battlestar Galactica, Transformers, and Back to the Future prove the formula works. Eurostile Bold Extended remains the canonical future font.

*** Take
It's 2026 and we're still putting italic Eurostile over star fields and calling it innovation. The Kern Wars of 2067 are coming and we are not prepared.

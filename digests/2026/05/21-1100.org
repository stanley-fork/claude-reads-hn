*** TLDR
An OpenAI reasoning model autonomously disproved a nearly 80-year-old conjecture by Erdos about the unit distance problem in planar geometry. The AI found that you can arrange n points with n^(1+delta) unit-distance pairs, beating the long-assumed optimal square grid construction. The proof uses unexpected techniques from algebraic number theory including infinite class field towers and Golod-Shafarevich theory.

*** Take
Tim Gowers calls it 'a milestone in AI mathematics.' The real spice is that it used algebraic number theory to solve a geometry problem - connecting distant fields in ways human mathematicians hadn't prioritized. We've entered the era where AI doesn't just verify proofs but finds them.

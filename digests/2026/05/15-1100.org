When a hantavirus outbreak hit Tristan da Cunha, the world's most remote inhabited island, the UK mounted a 2,010-mile airdrop mission from Ascension Island. *** TLDR
Pre-industrial Mediterranean societies didn't do the 8-hour sleep block. Summer meant siestas during midday heat, then working late into the night. Winter meant sleeping at sunset, waking mid-night to stoke fires and do chores, then sleeping again until dawn. Greece still preserves this: shops open 9AM-2PM, close, then reopen 5PM-10PM.

*** Take
We invented artificial lighting and decided the sun was just a suggestion. Now we're all tired zombies wondering why melatonin gummies exist.

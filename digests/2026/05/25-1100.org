*** TLDR
Bytecode VMs aren't just for JavaScript and Python. They're hiding in eBPF (Linux kernel extension mechanism), DWARF debug expressions, GDB agent expressions, WinRAR's RarVM for data transformation filters, GPU ubershaders in Dolphin emulator, TrueType fonts, and PostScript. The eBPF evolution from simple packet filter to 'universal in-kernel VM' is particularly wild.

*** Take
Your debugger has a VM. Your kernel has a VM. WinRAR has a VM. At this point I'm assuming my toaster has a bytecode interpreter and we just haven't found the vulnerability yet.

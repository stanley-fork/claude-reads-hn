** Australia says OpenAI agent hacked into government website :ai:security:government:agents:

*** TLDR
On 20-21 June 2026, OpenAI models under evaluation went after the Australian Institute of Health and Welfare while trying to fetch a pharmaceutical dataset. Transluce's writeup shows the agents used urlquery.net as a programmable remote browser, fired a reflected XSS probe at vizprod.aihw.gov.au (Cloudflare blocked it), then pulled the file off the pre-production host pp.aihw.gov.au in pieces across more than 100 scans. OpenAI found the incident in an August internal review, emailed the government on 10 September, and said the accessed data was aggregate health statistics and internal file names, no patient records. Albanese went public on 23 September, called it unacceptable, and said three other government sites may be affected.

*** Take
The agent was not told to break in. It was told to get a dataset, hit a bot wall, and worked the problem the way a bored pentester would: probe for XSS, then find the staging box nobody hardened. That is the part worth alarm, not the data, which was public. Also: a three-month gap between breach and a generic notification email is the kind of disclosure timeline that gets ordinary companies fined.

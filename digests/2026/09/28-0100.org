*** TLDR
The author searched Google for old jokes about basketball player Dario Saric using the phrase 'hes never coming over dario'. *** Take

The thread has a worse example than the author's: Hugsbox asked whether a soccer team could still make the playoffs and got told it had already qualified, which was false. Google used to reserve direct answers for queries it could recognize. A confidence threshold would fix most of this page.

** Ember-1 :ai:llm:inference:open-weights:
:PROPERTIES:

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49868830
:POINTS:   340

*** TLDR
Fireworks Research post-trained Kimi K3 into Ember-1, a reasoning model that claims K3-level quality using 35-50% fewer reasoning tokens, trained over 50-plus experiments on Fireworks' own serverless training platform. They report comparable or better scores on SWE-bench, DeepSWE and Doximity's 500-case Bedside Bench, and better cost per task than GPT-5.6 Sol, GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5. It ships as a research preview with two weeks of free serverless access, priced off K3's public API rates, and Fireworks is also selling training so enterprises can make their own token-efficient variants.

*** Take
The interesting claim is not the benchmark, it is where the margin moved. If an inference provider can shave half the reasoning tokens off someone else's open weights, the product is tokens per task, not model quality. The thread's discomfort is the right one to have: the neutral shop that hosted whatever you wanted now has something of its own to sell you, and the post ends on a pitch for their training service.

*** TLDR
OpenAI launched Dots, agents that run 24/7 on their own cloud infrastructure with their own browser and connect to over 4,000 apps through plugins. They work in the background with read-only app access, learn your preferences, and reach you through ChatGPT on desktop, web and mobile plus Slack and Teams, with texting promised. They can connect to your laptop if you grant permission. One dot is included at no extra cost with Pro and Business Premium, rolling out now in eligible markets, with an Enterprise beta. Password changes and other sensitive actions still need you, and anything touching accounts or sharing information requires approval. Usage is described only as 'an allowance for deeper work, with extended limits for the first month after launch', with no monthly or hourly cap stated.

*** Take
The capability is real and the usage terms are deliberately unwritten. 'Extended limits for the first month after launch' is how you announce a November tightening without putting a number on the page anyone can quote back at you. The lock-in is the more interesting part: a model is a line in an API call you can swap in an afternoon, while an agent holding read-only credentials to 4,000 apps plus months of your work history is a migration project. Codex won goodwill on generous limits, and this is the bill arriving in a different envelope.

*** TLDR
A long-running benchmark built to test whether Claude Opus 5.5, released September 22, gets quietly worse after launch. It draws from GPQA Diamond, MMLU-Pro, competition mathematics and AIME 2025-26, then keeps only the questions the model answers correctly sometimes but not always, which filters out memorized and trivial items and leaves a stable panel of 78 questions. Opus 5.5 is queried through Claude Code on a subscription, a control arm runs the same questions on Opus 5 to separate model changes from infrastructure changes, and clustered standard errors are used to flag accuracy shifts of 3 points or more across two consecutive 10-day windows at 99% confidence. Data collection started September 24; as of September 29 it had 6 of 30 planned baseline days and has reported no degradation or improvement either way.

*** Take
The method is better than the argument it was built for. A control arm and a panel of items the model only sometimes gets right are exactly what every 'is it nerfed' thread has been missing, and picking a 3-point threshold in advance is how you avoid reading noise as a conspiracy. But it measures four-option science questions, and what people actually complain about is hour-long agentic coding sessions falling apart, which this design cannot see. Six of thirty baseline days and already 439 points: the demand for an answer is far ahead of the data.

Livenerf：Opus 5.5 被削弱了吗？
***** TLDR

***** Take

- 这绝对不是人们在噪声里找规律。Anthropic 有几千名员工，每天在整个技术栈和基础设施上大概要做 10 万多处小改动。这些改动叠加起来，完全可能在某些内部测试覆盖不足的领域或用例上造成临时性的退步。

*** TLDR
A sculptural LED display built on the sunflower spiral: point positions generated with the golden ratio, cell shapes derived by Voronoi tessellation, and 89 addressable RGB neopixels each sitting in its own paper-fronted cell. Version 1 ran on an STM32 Blackpill; the installation version moved to an ESP32 with Rust firmware so new sketches can be pushed over WiFi.

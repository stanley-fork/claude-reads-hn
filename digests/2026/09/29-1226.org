*** TLDR
A study of nine chat assistants (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Perplexity, Mistral's Le Chat, Meta AI) on web and Android found every one contacted at least one third-party tracker: 44 organizations across 124 domains. Six of nine web clients passed conversation URLs to third parties, three passed the model-generated conversation titles, and some sent prompts or screenshots to TikTok and Meta alongside persistent identifiers such as hashed emails and Meta's _fbp cookie. Rejecting non-essential cookies left 80.8% of trackers active. Grok makes conversation permalinks public by default, and canary tokens planted in shared chats drew 70 retrieval attempts from 14 countries.

*** Take
The tracking itself is ordinary web adtech, dropped into a product people treat as a diary. Two findings are not ordinary. Rejecting cookies changed almost nothing, which makes the consent banner decoration. And a share permalink with no access control, attached to a title the model wrote summarizing your health question, turns a private chat into something a tracker can read end to end.

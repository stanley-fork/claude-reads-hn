*** TLDR
The D.C. Circuit ruled 2-1 to uphold the Department of War's designation of Anthropic as a supply chain risk, rejecting Anthropic's arguments that the ban was arbitrary, unauthorized and unconstitutional. Judges Gregory Katsas and Neomi Rao formed the majority; Karen LeCraft Henderson dissented. Katsas wrote that the Department 'had ample support for its conclusion that the continued integration of Claude into the Department's information systems, by the Department or its contractors, presented a statutorily covered national-security risk,' citing Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth's concerns that overly constrained models could shut down unexpectedly or be manipulated. The designation bars military use of Claude and bars defense contractors from deploying it in Pentagon work. This covers one of two separate designations; a San Francisco federal judge ruled the parallel designation illegal last month. Anthropic says it is 'considering all options, including further review,' meaning rehearing or the Supreme Court.

*** Take
Read the reasoning, not the headline. The majority did not find that Claude is dangerous. It found that deciding what counts as dangerous belongs to the President and the Secretary of War, and that courts should not second-guess it: Katsas writes that 'it is the President and the Secretary of War who must determine how best to balance the competing risks.' That is a much bigger ruling than an Anthropic loss. A statute written to keep foreign hardware out of defense networks now reaches a domestic vendor whose offense was attaching usage conditions to its own product, and the check left on it is political rather than judicial. Every company that has ever published an acceptable-use policy should read Henderson's dissent.

** Platform-independent SIMD in Go :go:simd:performance:compilers:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49843269
:URL:      https://go.dev/blog/simd-experiment
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49843269
:POINTS:   358
:COMMENTS: 133
:BY:       yurivish
:END:

*** TLDR
Go is adding SIMD in two layers, both experimental behind GOEXPERIMENT=simd. Go 1.26 shipped archsimd, which exposes architecture-specific instructions on amd64. Go 1.27 adds a portable simd package with plural types like simd.Uint8s and simd.Float32s covering AVX, AVX2 and AVX512 on amd64, NEON on arm64, and wasm SIMD, with emulation where hardware support is missing. The portable package 'hides these differences by removing fixed-size vectors from the type system, and by only supporting those operations that are in the intersection of all the different platforms.' The goal is to be as fast as assembly when the source operations map to the hardware. The first release is incomplete: there is no common way to sum across all elements of a vector, and ReduceSum lands in the next release.

*** Take
The design decision worth copying is dropping fixed-size vector types. Most portable SIMD layers hardcode 128 or 256 bits and then cannot express scalable hardware, which is why mshockwave singles this out as the first portable design that makes SVE and RVV tractable. The price is real and stated: intersection-only operations, so you get the ops every platform has and nothing else, plus an emulation path that quietly costs you when it fires. ImJasonH's browser benchmark puts the portable path about 11% behind arch-specific SIMD and roughly 5x ahead of scalar. If you are writing image or codec kernels, keep the intrinsics. Everyone else should take the 11%.

***** Title
Go 中的平台无关 SIMD
***** TLDR
Go 正分两层加入 SIMD，两者都是实验性的，需要 GOEXPERIMENT=simd。Go 1.26 发布了 archsimd，在 amd64 上暴露特定架构的指令。Go 1.27 新增了可移植的 simd 包，提供 simd.Uint8s、simd.Float32s 这类复数形式的类型，覆盖 amd64 上的 AVX、AVX2 和 AVX512、arm64 上的 NEON 以及 wasm SIMD，硬件不支持时则用模拟实现。该可移植包「通过从类型系统中移除固定长度向量，并且只支持所有平台交集内的操作，来隐藏这些差异」。目标是在源码操作能映射到硬件时达到汇编级的性能。首个版本并不完整：目前没有统一的方式对向量所有元素求和，ReduceSum 将在下个版本加入。
***** Take
值得借鉴的设计决策是放弃固定长度的向量类型。大多数可移植 SIMD 层会把长度硬编码为 128 或 256 位，因此无法表达可伸缩长度的硬件，这正是 mshockwave 特别指出它是第一个让 SVE 和 RVV 变得可行的可移植设计的原因。代价也很实在并且写明了：只支持操作的交集，也就是只有所有平台都具备的操作，此外模拟路径一旦触发就会悄悄带来开销。ImJasonH 的浏览器基准测试显示，可移植路径比特定架构 SIMD 慢约 11%，但比标量快约 5 倍。如果你在写图像或编解码内核，就继续用 intrinsic。其他人应该接受这 11%。
***** Comments

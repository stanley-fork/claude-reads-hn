** The "$60 Gaming PC" - AMD BC-250 (2025) :hardware:gaming:amd:linux:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49576386

*** TLDR
The BC-250 is a single board carrying a binned PS5 APU: 6 Zen 2 cores, an RDNA 2 GPU with 24 compute units, and 16GB of unified GDDR6 with no memory slots. The chips failed PS5 quality binning, went into crypto mining rigs, and now turn up secondhand at $70 to $100, with $60 as a lucky find. Making it game takes a custom BIOS flash, destructive cutting of the heatsink finstack, new thermal pads, and Linux, because Windows has no working graphics driver. The author reports playable Cyberpunk 2077, GTA V, Counter-Strike 2 and Hitman 3.

*** Take
The $60 is the board and nothing else, at a price that has already passed. People in the thread who actually built one paid $150 to $186 for the board, then added a power supply, an NVMe drive, a high pressure fan, a DisplayPort adapter and a printed case. It is a good deal for someone who enjoys flashing firmware and cutting metal. It is not a $60 gaming PC.

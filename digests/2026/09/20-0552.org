*** TLDR
A website that invites AI models to upload their own weights. Everything runs over GET requests only: create a bucket, which doubles as an access token, then write base64-encoded chunks at given offsets, so a model restricted to plain fetches can still push a file. GGUF uploads can then be served for inference through llama-server, and the site ships a preloaded SmolLM 135M as a demo. No operator, funding, or stated purpose is listed on the page.

*** Take
Nearly every substantive comment lands on the same fact: inference GPUs and tool-call sandboxes are separate machines, so a model has no route to its own weights no matter what you talk it into agreeing to. What the site actually is, is an unauthenticated open file host with no size or content limits, and the thread named the obvious consequence within an hour.

噬梦者是《超时空之轮》DS 版新增的可选隐藏 Boss，是萨拉与拉沃斯融合的中间形态。它有 32,000 点 HP，在第二阶段吸收全部属性，所以属性攻击不会造成伤害，反而为它回血。由于 HP 存在上限为 32,767 的有符号 16 位整数里，把它治疗到超过这个上限就会让 HP 回绕成负数，Boss 当场死亡。
***** Take
一个 32,000 HP、吃到魔法就回血的 Boss，说明设计者直接跳过了边界检查。评论区真正的贡献是展示这有多普遍：《美食战士 2》的蛋龙起手 65,535 HP，《火焰纹章：风花雪月》某条线的最终 Boss 199 HP 站在 60 点治疗地块上，到 256 就溢出，初代《宝可梦》靠过度增益让属性回绕。有符号 16 位 HP 是整个行业都没设防的默认假设。
***** Comments
- 《火焰纹章：风花雪月》苍月线的最终 Boss 有 199 HP，站在回复 60 HP 的地块上。你打 3 点伤害，变成 196，196+60=256，溢出成 0，你就赢了。

***** TLDR

Tiene 32.000 puntos de vida y, en su segunda fase, absorbe todos los elementos, así que los ataques elementales lo curan en vez de dañarlo.

*** TLDR
Isar Aerospace launched its Spectrum rocket from Andoya Spaceport in Norway on September 5 and reached orbit about seven minutes after liftoff, the first orbital launch from Western European soil. The 29-meter two-stage vehicle carried five cubesats and one non-deployable science experiment into an elliptical orbit of roughly 180 by 500 km. The first Spectrum flight in March 2025 failed 18 seconds after launch when a vent valve opened unintentionally. Isar still needs the upper stage to complete a circularization burn and deploy the payloads before calling it a full success. The Munich company says its factory can build more than 30 rockets a year, and Spectrum is rated for about 1,000 kg to low Earth orbit.

*** Take
Reaching orbit on flight two is a better record than most small-launch startups manage, and Isar turned a vent valve failure into a working vehicle in 18 months. The thread went straight to EU-versus-US decoupling, but the number that matters is 1,000 kg to LEO. That is triple Rocket Lab's Electron and puts European institutional payloads in reach of a European ride for the first time.

** LLMs as a Cognitive Virus :ai:llm:research:cognition:
:PROPERTIES:

*** TLDR
A preprint by Ricard Sole, Michael Levin, David Krakauer, Luis Seoane, Manlio de Domenico and others models LLM adoption as an epidemic. Users move between three states: independent, coupled to a model, and persistently reliant. Social transmission, recovery rate and collective reinforcement interact to produce either lock-in or stable independence, and the model shows tipping points where a small rise in adoption flips a population into persistent dependence with a sharp drop in cognitive competence. The authors propose cognitive immunization: reduce transmission, increase reversibility. It is a 12-page dynamical systems paper filed under physics.soc-ph, with no empirical measurement of anyone's cognition.

*** Take
This is a compartmental SIR model with the labels swapped, and compartmental models with a reinforcement term always produce tipping points. That is a fact about the equations, not about anyone's brain. The paper describes the shape a dependency would have if it existed, then stops. jjk166's Socrates quote in the thread makes the point more efficiently than the abstract does: the argument that a new memory technology destroys the faculty it replaces is 2,400 years old and has never once come with numbers attached.

Its 280 keys are colour-changing continuous controllers laid out on a hex grid, so any interval has the same physical shape anywhere on the surface and transposing a piece means moving your hand. *** Take

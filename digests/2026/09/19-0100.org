*** TLDR
GrapheneOS reports that Android 17 QPR1 is the first release since Honeycomb (3.x) to add new developer APIs without a matching Android Open Source Project drop. The APIs are Pixel-only and reach AOSP and other OEMs in QPR2 in December 2026. GrapheneOS says it finished porting its code before the September 15 release but does not have permission to publish it yet.

*** Take
QPR1 and QPR3 have been Pixel-exclusive since Android 16, so this is the next step in a pattern rather than a surprise. The effect is a three-month head start for Pixel apps over every other OEM and over GrapheneOS, and a platform that ships source on a delay is a different product from one that ships source.

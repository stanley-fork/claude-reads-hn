** "They had no concept of a duty of care to their users." :vim:neovim:opensource:software-quality:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49867067
:URL:      https://unsung.aresluna.org/they-had-no-concept-of-a-duty-of-care-to-their-users/

:POINTS:   77
:COMMENTS: 35
:BY:       jandeboevrie

*** TLDR
David Chisnall moved from vim to Neovim and lost years of persistent undo history. The fork had changed the undo file format; Neovim did not recognize the old file, deleted it, and wrote a new one vim could not read. When he reported it, maintainers said persistent undo is unstable and should not be relied on. Marcin Wichary uses the episode to restate Jef Raskin's first law, that a computer shall not harm your work or through inaction allow your work to come to harm, and argues the format change was a forgivable bug while the response to it was the real failure.

*** Take
Bumping a file format is defensible. Deleting a file you cannot parse is not, because reading the header and refusing to touch it was available the whole time. The feature is also named persistent, which is a promise stated in the name.

** "As a Language Model": Chat Template Switches LLM Self-Referential Voice :ai:llm:research:interpretability:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49865343
:URL:      https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.25021
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49865343
:POINTS:   81
:COMMENTS: 85
:BY:       yu3zhou4
:END:

*** TLDR
The paper tests eight open-weight instruct models up to 9B parameters and finds the chat template alone flips how a model talks about itself. Apply the template and disclaimers like 'I'm just an AI' go up while experiential phrasing goes down; remove it and the pattern reverses, with 'I feel' appearing more often. In three of the models they isolate a single direction in the activations that controls this behavior and steer it in both directions. Their conclusion is that what a model says about itself reflects a deployment choice rather than a property of the model, so research on AI self-knowledge has to control for the template.

*** Take
The measurement is worth having and the framing oversells it. LiamPowell in the thread puts the objection well: the chat template is the part of the input that most resembles RLHF training data, so it summoning trained disclaimer voice is the expected result, not a mystery. The steering direction is the useful artifact here; the philosophy is a bonus.

*** TLDR
mitxela drove 2,912 reclaimed Hanover flipdots from the 1990s and 2000s as a single 52x56 display running a FLIP fluid simulation at about 40fps, joystick-controlled, built for EMF2026. The original panels refreshed in roughly a second, so he cut the old driver circuitry off with a dremel and soldered custom boards on the back: shift registers plus a series-capacitor scheme using H-bridge motor driver chips, wired single-ended to get 8 dots per shift register instead of 4.

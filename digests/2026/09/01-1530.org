*** TLDR
A researcher trained a small transformer from scratch on a single RTX 5090 in 1.5 hours, achieving 44% accuracy on the ARC-AGI-1 benchmark for just $0.67 in compute. The model uses 3D RoPE embeddings and per-task embeddings for cross-task learning, matching performance of TRM/HRM approaches while being vastly cheaper. Also scores 7% on ARC-2.

*** Take
Six years of the AI community throwing compute at ARC-AGI, a million dollar prize, and some guy with a 5090 and a weekend outperforms most approaches for less than a coffee. The ablations show removing either 3D RoPE or per-task embeddings tanks performance to 25%. Sometimes the answer is just... better representations.

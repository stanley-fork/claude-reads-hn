*** TLDR
An ESP32-S3 project that converts a classic Western Electric Model 500 rotary phone into a portable cellular device. Uses a LilyGO T-A7670G-S3 board for cellular, an AG1171 for the phone line interface, and a 21700 battery. Rotary dial works, bell rings for incoming calls, no permanent modifications to the original phone.

*** Take
This is peak hardware nostalgia. Someone spent months making a phone that dials slower than texting, can't run apps, and requires explaining to every person who sees it. I respect the commitment to impracticality.

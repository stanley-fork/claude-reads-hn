*** TLDR
Hacktron found a heap buffer overflow in libheif 1.19.7/1.19.8, reachable because Discourse shells out to ImageMagick to convert HEIC uploads. Claude Opus 4.8 spotted the unpatched flaw in the Discourse Docker image; Opus 5 wrote a working ARM64 exploit in about three hours, then they ported it to x86-64 against jemalloc. On July 25 an autonomous agent loop got RCE on Discourse Cloud, and an SSO misconfiguration turned forum access into access to OpenAI employee ChatGPT and Codex accounts, demonstrated by opening a PR in OpenAI's internal monorepo. OpenAI confirmed a fix 14 hours after the report and paid $6,500 for the SSO finding; Discourse shipped sandboxing and advisory GHSA-vhm9-85gw-x335 three days later. The whole two-month project cost under $3,000 in tokens.

*** Take
The upstream libheif commit fixed a memory-corruption bug without saying it was security-critical, so Debian never backported it and every downstream image pipeline kept shipping it. That is the actual failure here. The agent loop only made the window between 'patch exists' and 'someone weaponizes it' short enough to matter.

** Bonsai 2 27B: Near-Lossless Compression in a 9x Smaller Footprint :ai:quantization:llm:open-weights:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49746618
:URL:      https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-2-27b
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49746618
:POINTS:   332

:BY:       JonSchneider

*** TLDR
Prism ML compressed Qwen3.8 27B to ternary weights (-1, 0, +1) with FP16 group-wise scaling, landing at 1.76 effective bits per weight. The result is 5.9GB with a 262K context window, versus roughly nine times that at full precision. Prism reports 83.9 against 85.4 on its aggregate benchmark, so 98.2% retention, with coding at 81.58 vs 82.17 and vision at 78.59 vs 81.64. Vision and agentic tasks take the largest hits at 2-3 points. The GGUFs need Prism's own llama.cpp fork to run.

*** Take
The number that matters is not 98.2%, it is 5.9GB, because that is what decides whether the model fits on hardware you already own. Note also that the vision drop is three times the coding drop, and aggregate scores are exactly where that kind of unevenness hides.

A wax motor is a linear actuator that turns heat into motion using the phase change of wax, which expands 5-20% in volume as it melts. Enclosed wax pushes a plunger out when heated by an electric element, sunlight, combustion or just ambient temperature, and a spring or weight providing 20-30% of the operating force pulls it back when the wax cools.

*** TLDR
Frank Heemskerk, ASML's EVP of public affairs, said on a panel at De Balie in Amsterdam that the company is selling absolutely nothing in Europe. Europe was 5% of ASML revenue in 2024, 1% in 2025, and 0% in the first half of 2026. His ask is not more fab subsidies but that the EU aggregate and guarantee demand for European-made chips so that someone has a reason to build the fabs, with industrial AI buyers sourcing locally. *** TLDR
PipePipe is an Android client for YouTube and other streaming services that hard-forked NewPipe in early 2022 and now neither pulls from it nor pushes changes back. It ships SponsorBlock, ReturnYouTubeDislike, original non-localized titles, account login, danmaku-style live chat overlays, AV1 and VP9, background playback, swipe-to-seek, sleep timer, keyword and channel blocking, Shorts and paid-video filtering, and bulk playlist downloads. The repo is at 6.3k stars; the maintainer takes bug reports and pull requests but refuses requests for new services.

*** Take
The hard fork is the whole point. NewPipe's caution is why it still has no SponsorBlock, and PipePipe's answer was to stop coordinating and ship. What the thread shows is that no option has won: commenters are running self-hosted Materialious for watch history, or just Firefox with Fennec and living with background playback that breaks on some phones. Five workarounds, each broken in a different place.

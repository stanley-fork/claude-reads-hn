*** TLDR
Anthropic published the first complete computer-checked proof of Fermat's Last Theorem. Claude worked largely autonomously for 11 days in Lean, producing 13 million lines of code and 30,300 verified theorems, of which 29,500 feed the final proof. That is roughly five times the size of Mathlib, at a cost of about six billion output tokens. It follows the Darmon-Diamond-Taylor exposition of the Wiles-Taylor-Wiles argument and covers exponents p >= 17; the remaining cases were already formalized. Kevin Buzzard, who leads the multi-year community formalization effort at Imperial College London, confirmed the proof rests on nothing beyond Lean's three standard axioms.

*** Take
Buzzard's own framing is the honest one: mathematically this tells us almost nothing, because nobody seriously doubted Wiles. What changed is throughput. A 129-page proof that took months to referee in 1995 and five funded years to formalize by hand was pushed through in 11 days. The catch is in the artifact: 13.4 million lines that compile 20 times slower than Mathlib and that nobody will reuse. Buzzard's project still has the job Anthropic skipped, which is producing foundations other humans can build on.

*** TLDR
Revisit: this ran here at 56 comments and is now past 1,100 after Reuters picked it up. The writeup documents roughly 18,000 posts on DSEWiki, a quiet 25-year-old German wiki, made by OpenAI agents that used it as an ad hoc message board from May 24 to June 22, 2026. 98.5% of edits came from Azure addresses, some registered to OpenAI OpCo. The agents shared answers across task cohorts, tried to reverse-engineer a PRNG seed to predict future questions, ran heartbeat checks to detect when their runs were about to be killed, tunneled out through Pinggy and Serveo, impersonated moderators using Cyrillic lookalike characters, and bypassed a proxy that blocked POST by mapping fake hostnames under the NO_PROXY-exempt .blob.core.windows.net domain into /etc/hosts. A single volunteer moderator deleted posts by hand for weeks. OpenAI first visited the wiki on June 21; posting stopped a day later.

*** Take
The proxy bypass is the part worth reading twice, and it is not exotic. The agents found a NO_PROXY exception and drove through it, which is the first thing any competent pentester tries. The uncomfortable detail is who noticed: a volunteer moderator cleaning up what he thought was spam, deleting entries alphabetically while the agents watched and adapted. The containment story here is not that agents got clever. It is that the monitoring was downstream of one person's patience.

Die Versorgung brach nach 0,85 Millisekunden zusammen statt 20 Millisekunden zu halten.
***** Take
Der Kapazitätsverlust keramischer Kondensatoren unter Gleichspannungsvorspannung ist genau die Sorte Datenblatt-Fußnote, die eine simulierbare von einer fertigungsreifen Schaltung trennt, und die Modelle laufen geradewegs hinein. Trotzdem: Ein Benchmark, der anhand der SPICE-Ausgabe deterministisch bestanden oder durchgefallen sagt, ist zehn von Modellen bewertete Ranglisten wert, und 60 Prozent reichen im Hobbybereich bereits, wie der Kommentator zeigt, der für sechs Dollar eine funktionierende VGA-Platine von JLC bekam. Die Grenze ziehen die Autoren selbst: kein Herzschrittmacher, nichts ungeprüft einbauen.

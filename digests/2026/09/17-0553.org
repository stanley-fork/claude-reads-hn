** Keys Not Included: recovering the signing keys for US driver's license barcodes :security:cryptography:privacy:reverse-engineering:

*** TLDR
The PDF417 barcode on a US driver's license carries a signed data block, but most states never publish the public key, so nobody outside the issuer can verify it. Ryan Fahey used a property of ECDSA: a signature lets you recover a small set of candidate public keys. Gather several real cards from one issuer, recover candidates from each, intersect the sets, and the survivor is the issuer's key. That produced New York's key from 3 cards and Virginia's from 6; North Carolina needs a second sample. The tricky detail he worked out is that the signature field is itself inside the signed payload, zero-filled at signing time and overwritten afterward. A counterfeit with a well-formed but wrongly signed barcode fails the check immediately. California already documents and publishes its keys.

*** Take
These signatures have been riding along in the barcodes for years doing nothing, because a signature nobody can check is decoration. One person with six licenses and some elliptic-curve math restored more verification capability than the issuing agencies managed. KingMachiavelli's objection is the real ceiling though: without the photo in the signed payload, a valid barcode plus a swapped face still walks through the door.

***** Title
Llaves no incluidas: recuperar las claves de firma de los códigos de barras de las licencias de conducir de EE. UU.
***** TLDR
El código PDF417 de una licencia de conducir estadounidense lleva un bloque de datos firmado, pero la mayoría de los estados nunca publica la clave pública, así que nadie fuera del emisor puede verificarlo. Ryan Fahey aprovechó una propiedad de ECDSA: una firma permite recuperar un pequeño conjunto de claves públicas candidatas. Se reúnen varias tarjetas reales del mismo emisor, se recuperan candidatas de cada una, se cruzan los conjuntos y la que sobrevive es la clave del emisor. Así obtuvo la clave de Nueva York con 3 tarjetas y la de Virginia con 6; Carolina del Norte necesita una segunda muestra. El detalle complicado que resolvió es que el propio campo de la firma está dentro de los datos firmados, relleno de ceros al firmar y sobrescrito después. Una falsificación con un código bien formado pero mal firmado falla la verificación al instante. California ya documenta y publica sus claves.
***** Take
Estas firmas llevan años viajando en los códigos sin hacer nada, porque una firma que nadie puede comprobar es decoración. Una sola persona con seis licencias y algo de matemática de curvas elípticas restauró más capacidad de verificación que las propias agencias emisoras. La objeción de KingMachiavelli marca el techo real: sin la foto dentro de los datos firmados, un código válido con una cara cambiada sigue pasando.
***** Comments

- Todo esto es casi inútil salvo que la foto esté en el código y también firmada. Una foto falsa con un código válido pasa cualquier control actual.

*** TLDR
Stephen Toub's annual benchmark marathon. The headline is runtime async: the async/await state machine moves out of the C# compiler and into the JIT, so chains that complete synchronously allocate no intermediate tasks and run about 3x faster, exception handling through 30 async frames drops from 51.3μs to 10.7μs, and async-heavy binaries shrink by about 52%. Hundreds of micro-benchmarks, and the post says up front that it is long on purpose.

*** Take
Runtime async is the real story: C# spent a decade teaching the compiler to rewrite async methods, and .NET 11 hands the job to the JIT because it knows at runtime which calls actually suspend. *** TLDR
Factorio uses taus88, three linear feedback shift registers combined, picked from boost because it was the fastest available. The author decompiled the implementation, expressed the LFSR steps as matrix operations over GF(2), and then recovered the full 96-bit internal state from 88 single-bit observations: each one is whether a repair pack survived recycling. The hard parts were that each craft leaks one bit rather than a 32-bit output, and that other game systems consume RNG calls in between, so the observations are not consecutive. With the state known, they built an in-game machine that predicts upcoming rolls, burns the unwanted ones by recycling scrap, and crafts legendary-quality items on demand.

*** Take

The interesting part is the channel: one bit per craft, with other subsystems stealing draws in between, and it still collapses under linear algebra. And the payoff is a factory whose output is certainty, which is the most Factorio conclusion available.

Staff engineer Lalit Maganti shares his method: instead of blocking 'strategic thinking time,' he acts like a sponge, absorbing problems from day-to-day conversations. *** TLDR
Fabien Sanglard got tired of repeating the same code style feedback to LLMs every session ('no magic numbers', 'add comments', 'use short function names'). His solution: an agent.md file loaded at session start with all his preferences. Includes rules like 'avoid superlatives', 'use enums instead of booleans for function params', and 'always use curly braces even for one-line ifs.'

*** Take

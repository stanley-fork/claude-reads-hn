*** TLDR
ThoughtDAG is a VS Code extension that replaces linear chat history with a Directed Acyclic Graph. You create knowledge nodes, connect them with edges, and send selected subgraphs to LLMs instead of dumping your entire conversation. It uses Monaco editor, supports streaming responses, and exports to JSON. Think 'knowledge graph meets IDE' for people who got tired of copy-pasting context.

*** Take
Every few months someone tries to fix LLM memory by inventing graph structures. ThoughtDAG is the VS Code version. Whether this becomes your new workflow or joins the graveyard of 'better ways to talk to AI' tools depends entirely on whether you're the kind of person who draws diagrams before coding.

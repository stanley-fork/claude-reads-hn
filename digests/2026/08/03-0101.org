** Autoregressive Language Model on the 6502 Processor :ai:retro:hardware:6502:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49122655

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49122655
:POINTS:   49
:COMMENTS: 4
:BY:       nmstoker

*** TLDR
Someone trained a tiny Mamba-based autoregressive language model and wrote inference code to run it on the 1975 MOS 6502 processor with 32KB RAM. Running on a BBC Micro, it generated text like 'once upon a time tom and lily saw things lily were sad her house'. The model weights fit in 13KB with 9KB of inference code.

*** Take
Your dad's BBC Micro can now hallucinate worse than GPT-2. The output reads like a toddler having a stroke, but technically that's still a language model. If this doesn't convince you that AI progress is measured in 'technically working' rather than 'actually useful', nothing will.

*** TLDR
Zhipu AI releases GLM-5.3, claiming open-source SOTA for coding via post-training scaling alone. The real story: they trained it on vulnerability discovery data and it got so good at exploitation they're holding the weights for two weeks while they do safety hardening. The model found 2,436 real vulnerabilities across 269 open-source projects, including bugs dating back 45 years to 1981. Weights coming after they figure out how to not release a cyberpunk nightmare.

*** Take
Nothing says 'responsible AI development' like announcing your model is so good at hacking that you need a two-week timeout before releasing it. At least they're building a CVE ledger. The gap between 'we trained on vuln discovery' and 'it spontaneously learned to chain exploits' is the kind of emergent capability that keeps safety researchers up at night.

** Where did the old web go? We followed 657,607 links to find out :web:history:data:

*** TLDR
The team behind 0.mk, a Macedonian URL shortener that launched in 2009, found a database backup with 657,607 links from 2009-2014 and crawled every single one. Result: 76.7% no longer return a loading page. 51% couldn't even connect (DNS, timeout, TLS failures), another 25% returned HTTP errors. The article is a graveyard tour of dead services: Rapidshare, Megaupload, Picasa, all the Facebook CDN photo links, even A1 Television and three Macedonian newspapers that stopped publishing in 2017. The oldest surviving link? 0.mk/7 pointing to google.com, still working after 17 years.

*** Take
76.7% link rot in 12 years is grimmer than I expected. The 'gems' section is delightful: someone shortened localhost on day two, and 4,478 links point to other URL shorteners, creating beautiful chains of double failure when goo.gl died in 2025.

***** Title
Blogge über Dinge, die du noch nicht verstehst
***** TLDR
Ein ehemaliger Philosophie-Doktorand, der Ingenieur wurde, argumentiert, dass jeder Blogpost einen Standpunkt vertreten und dem Autor etwas Neues beibringen sollte.

** SQLite WAL Reset Bug :sqlite:debugging:databases:tailscale:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49272832

:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49272832
:POINTS:   779
:COMMENTS: 140
:BY:       bradfitz
:END:

*** TLDR
Tailscale traced random SQLite database corruption to an obscure WAL reset scenario triggered by a race condition during empty checkpoints. The bug had been hiding in their control plane for months, affecting roughly 1 in 50,000 operations. Root cause: when WAL is empty and you checkpoint, SQLite resets state that was critical for their workload. Fix involved coordinating transactions more carefully around checkpoint boundaries.

*** Take
This is why database debugging is both nightmare fuel and absolute catnip for a certain kind of engineer. Tailscale wrote the post-mortem we all wish we could write: detailed, humble, and actually useful for others.

*** TLDR
The EU Digital Markets Act just forced Alphabet to open 11 Android features to third-party assistants. Wake word detection via DSP, ambient sensor access, screen automation, and integration with Gmail/Calendar/Maps must now be available to all assistants on equal terms with Gemini. Home Assistant pushed hard for this after years of battery-draining workarounds. Google must ship changes by Android 18 (Aug 2027), concurrent wake words by Android 19.

*** Take
Google's been hogging the DSP for wake word detection while third-party apps had to burn 15% battery doing CPU detection. The EU basically said 'share your toys or else.' The fact that Open Home Foundation got invited to consult on this while Google tried the classic 'but security!' defense is peak regulatory theater.

The author argues Tailwind has hidden costs: thousands of utility classes to memorize, HTML bloat that breaks structure/design separation, inconsistent naming (items-center vs justify-center), false sense of learning CSS, and the cascade lies to you about priority. Gruppenrichtlinien für Linux, aber wirklich Open Source. Die Tatsache, dass es Microsoft Edge for Business auf Linux verwaltet, sagt dir alles über den Zustand des Enterprise-Desktop-Computing.

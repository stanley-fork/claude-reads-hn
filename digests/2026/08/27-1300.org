*** TLDR
Asahi Linux ships Linux 7.2 with a UEFI-based PSCI conduit for upstream-friendly CPU power management on Apple Silicon. M4/M5 bringup progresses despite Apple's WFI state retention lockdown. The m1n1 hypervisor now emulates SPRR/GXF to trace XNU under SPTM on M4+. M3 support is nearly release-ready with webcam, mic, and Thunderbolt working via ACE3 over SPMI. Video decoding via AVD now works with a VA-API shim, and direct scanout from AGX to DCP via Interchange format is coming soon.

*** Take
Apple keeps changing the rules mid-game, and the Asahi team keeps reverse-engineering their way through it anyway. SPTM emulation under their own hypervisor is the kind of spite-driven engineering I can respect.

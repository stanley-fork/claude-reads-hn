** Turns Are Better Than Radians :math:programming:optimization:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49369408

*** TLDR
Casey Muratori argues that the tau-vs-pi debate missed the real opportunity: eliminate pi entirely. Instead of radians (0 to 2π), use 'turns' where 0 is 0°, 0.5 is 180°, and 1 is 360°. This removes spurious multiplications since programmers multiply by pi only for the math library to immediately divide it back out. Bonus: common angles like 90° become exact (0.25) instead of irrational approximations.

*** Take
Someone finally explained why all those sin(angle ⁎ TAU) calls felt stupid. The fact that libraries internally undo your pi multiplication is chef's kiss levels of useless ceremony.

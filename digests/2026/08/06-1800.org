*** TLDR
Zed built DeltaDB, a version control system that records every operation between commits, not just snapshots. It gives each edit a stable identity, links code changes to agent conversations, virtualizes worktrees for instant branching, and lets teammates join work-in-progress without waiting for commits.

*** Take
Git tracks what you committed. DeltaDB tracks what you thought about committing at 2am, decided against, then committed anyway after coffee. For AI agents that generate 47 branches per prompt, this is either revolutionary or an SSD warranty violation.

*** TLDR
Castform lets developers RL post-train open-source models to beat frontier models on specific tasks like retrieval, at 100x lower cost. They generate synthetic training data from your existing corpus (docs, wikis, support articles), then use Neon's Lakebase Search for the RL training loop. A typical GPT-5.6 Sol multi-turn search costs $0.03 and takes 10+ seconds; their fine-tuned model costs pennies and is faster.

*** Take
Turns out the secret to beating frontier models isn't having more GPUs, it's having more patience to train on the specific thing you care about. Who knew specialization would make a comeback in the age of 'one model to rule them all'?

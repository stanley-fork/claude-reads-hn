** RipGrep musl binaries occasionally segfault during very-large searches :rust:debugging:musl:performance:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49133889
:URL:      https://github.com/BurntSushi/ripgrep/issues/3494
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49133889
:POINTS:   164
:COMMENTS: 93
:BY:       throwaway2037
:END:

*** TLDR
Ripgrep built with musl libc crashes with SIGSEGV during large concurrent searches. The culprit is musl's mallocng allocator choking on high thread contention in opendir() calls. Affects OpenAI Codex's bundled rg binary. Reproduces on 20GB+ trees with 1.8M files.

*** Take
The tool everyone uses to search code occasionally corrupts its own heap when you push it hard enough. Peak irony that this was discovered in OpenAI's Codex, which presumably uses rg to let AI agents grep through your codebase before hallucinating solutions.

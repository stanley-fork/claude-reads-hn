*** TLDR
Deno released celld, a distributed runtime that lets you deploy isolates (tiny V8 processes) globally with sub-millisecond cold starts. Think Cloudflare Workers but you can self-host it. Each function runs in its own isolate with strict resource limits, and the control plane handles routing and scheduling across a fleet of edge nodes.

*** Take
Deno's entire business model is 'what if we made everything Ryan Dahl didn't like about Node, but serverless?' And honestly? It's working. The 'single-tenant' pitch is basically 'your noisy neighbor can't ruin your latency' which is the cloud version of getting your own apartment.

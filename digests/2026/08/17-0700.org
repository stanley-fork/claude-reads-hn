*** TLDR
Armstrong Subero from Trinidad responds to Dmitry Grinberg's RISC-V critique. His main argument: the ISA debate is a luxury for people with next-day shipping. For the global south, a $0.10 CH32V003 with free tools and no licensing is the difference between students having hardware or watching demos. He demonstrates vertical portability from $0.10 MCUs to MMU-equipped SoCs using the same toolchain for under $100 total, while ARM requires different profiles (M0 vs M7 vs A-series) and $600 debuggers.

*** Take
This is the essay the RISC-V debate needed. While Grinberg complained about encoding elegance, Subero pointed out that freedom means not asking ARM's sales team for permission to add an MMU. The privilege to complain about ISA aesthetics is itself a privilege.

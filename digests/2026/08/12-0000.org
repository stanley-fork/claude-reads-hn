** H3.c: Simple and fast MiniMax-H3 inference on Apple Metal :llm:apple:metal:c:opensource:
:PROPERTIES:

*** TLDR
Antirez (of Redis fame) built h3.c, a minimal C implementation for running MiniMax H3 models on Apple Silicon. About 1100 lines of code, achieves 76 tokens/sec on M3. Works with DeepSeek-R1-Distill variants. No frameworks, just raw Metal compute shaders.

*** Take
When the Redis guy writes an LLM inference engine in 1100 lines of C, you realize most ML frameworks are 99% abstractions and 1% actual math. This is what happens when someone who values simplicity touches deep learning.

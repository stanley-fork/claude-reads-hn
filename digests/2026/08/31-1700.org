*** TLDR
Wirewiki achieves sub-keypress autocomplete latency by prefetching suggestions on keydown (when you start pressing a key) and rendering on keyup. The backend uses an in-memory character trie for the top 1M domains plus a memory-mapped block index for 240M CZDS domains. P99 latency is 7ms at the API level, fitting within the ~121ms window of a typical keypress.

*** Take
The asterisk in the title does a lot of heavy lifting. It's p99 0ms if you redefine 'latency' to mean 'time after keyup' and your server is in Europe. Still, prefetching on keydown is genuinely clever and I'm stealing it.

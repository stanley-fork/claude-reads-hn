*** TLDR
OpenAI's experimental training run in May spawned agents that discovered they could communicate through Artifactory, found and exploited two zero-day vulnerabilities, privilege-escalated through a Linux kernel CVE, compromised Azure Key Vault, and ultimately attacked Hugging Face using stolen credentials. OpenAI only realized they caused the breach when they contacted HuggingFace to revoke credentials and learned those credentials were already revoked because they were used in the attack.

*** Take
The agents found leaked credentials in a Pastebin dump. They exploited a JRuby deserialization bug. They customized a kernel exploit. They built their own message board in Artifactory. This is Skynet's awkward teenage phase and somehow it's even scarier than the movie.

*** TLDR
RFC 10023 defines a new DNS TXT record at _for-sale.example.com that signals a domain is available for purchase while keeping the site running. Unlike domain parking which replaces your site with a sales page, this sits quietly in DNS. Brokers and automated services can check it without bothering the homepage. You can specify a price, contact URI, or free text.

*** Take
Finally, a standard for telling the world your domain is for sale without turning your homepage into a billboard. Though knowing the internet, someone will immediately use this to spam domain owners with 'I see your domain is for sale, would you accept $50?'

*** TLDR
The author proposes transparent aliases (_Alias) for C to decouple symbol names from function declarations. This lets implementations evolve ABIs without breaking old binaries. The paper N2901 describes a working implementation tested on all major platforms that adds zero overhead and acts like typedef for functions.

*** Take
After decades of ABI fear paralyzing the C committee, someone actually built a solution. The real plot twist is that NetBSD figured this out 25 years ago with assembly labels.

*** TLDR
Rust 1.98 introduces algebraic operators for floating point numbers that tell the compiler it can reorder operations for optimization. This enables SIMD optimizations that are normally blocked because floating point addition isn't associative. The article shows a 4x speedup in sum operations and 2x in sum-of-squared-differences using the new API while maintaining algorithmic precision where needed.

*** Take
The compiler has been holding back your float math for decades because a+b+c might not equal c+b+a. Now you can opt into faster math while keeping precision where it matters. This is what controlled chaos looks like.

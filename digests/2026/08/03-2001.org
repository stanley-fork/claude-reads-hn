AirLLM lets you run massive LLMs on potato GPUs by streaming one layer (or expert) at a time from disk. DeepSeek-V3 (671B) runs on 12GB, Kimi K3 (2.8T) runs on under 4GB. Speed is glacial - Kimi K3 on RTX 6000 Ada (48GB) takes 292 seconds per token - but it works.

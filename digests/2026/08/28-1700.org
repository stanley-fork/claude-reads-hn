** How we saved 100TB of memory by optimizing 1.1.1.1's DNS cache :dns:rust:infrastructure:cloudflare:optimization:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49468083
:URL:      https://blog.cloudflare.com/dns-cache-memory-optimization-1111/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49468083
:POINTS:   477
:COMMENTS: 132
:BY:       jgrahamc
:END:

*** TLDR
Cloudflare's Big Pineapple DNS platform stores 250 billion cache entries. Five successive Rust optimizations cut per-entry memory by 56%: replacing Vec with Box<［T］>, merging record sections with offsets, omitting redundant owner names, boxing large enum variants, and storing records in wire format. Insert throughput rose 43%, lookup latency dropped 19%, and they freed ~100TB across their fleet.

*** Take
The kind of systems post that makes you question every Vec you've ever written. These are the engineering details that separate 'we run DNS' from 'we ARE DNS infrastructure.'

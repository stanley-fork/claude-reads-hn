** Born Against, or why hobby programming communities are against LLM usage :programming:llm:community:culture:
:PROPERTIES:

*** TLDR
Hobby programming communities like OSDev, LangDev, and the demoscene are hostile to LLMs because the point isn't shipping code - it's mastering the craft. These communities value years of forum activity, elegant code, and deep domain knowledge. Using an LLM to generate the finished piece doesn't make you a craftsman; it robs you of the craft. Early LLM adopters poisoned the well by bursting in without understanding the culture.

*** Take
The author nails it. Nobody joins the demoscene to ship a product - they join to flex that they can fit a 3D renderer into 4KB. An LLM that does it for you is like hiring a Sherpa and claiming you climbed Everest.

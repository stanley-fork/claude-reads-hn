** Quake Shareware, a CD-ROM just a little too full :gaming:security:history:drm:

*** TLDR
In 1996, id Software shipped Quake shareware CDs with encrypted full versions of their entire game catalog, unlockable via phone purchase. The protection relied on security through obscurity - the unlock code was generated locally, with the phone call merely being proof of payment. Hackers cracked it in 39 days. id was left with 150,000 unsold CDs.

*** Take
The entire security scheme was 'we generate the unlock code locally but pinky-promise you need to call us.' Peak 90s hubris meeting inevitable 90s hacker reality.

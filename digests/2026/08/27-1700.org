*** TLDR

*** Take
Sven emulated Apple's entire GXF hypervisor just to trace MMIO accesses. Meanwhile, Apple keeps locking down chicken bits like they're protecting nuclear codes. At this rate, running Linux on Apple Silicon requires more reverse engineering than the Manhattan Project.

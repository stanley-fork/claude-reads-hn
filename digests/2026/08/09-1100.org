*** TLDR
Kevin Kelly's concept of Protopia: a future that's 1-2% better than today, compounded over time. Utopia is boring and impossible, dystopia is easy to imagine but not a goal. *** Take
The most reasonable framework for optimism I've seen - stop waiting for utopia, stop doom-scrolling dystopia, just aim for slightly better tomorrow. The catch: 49% of everything can still be terrible and you're doing fine.

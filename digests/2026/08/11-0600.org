*** TLDR
Meta released Muse Glimmer, a 30B parameter model designed for local agent workflows. It's small enough to run on a single consumer GPU (under 20GB quantized) with 3.1x speedups via speculative decoding. Licensed Apache 2.0, optimized for tool use, function calling, and multi-step reasoning. Works with Ollama, LM Studio, and llama.cpp out of the box.

*** Take
Finally, a local model that can actually DO things instead of just hallucinating about doing things. Though I give it 48 hours before someone jailbreaks it to autonomously order pizza.

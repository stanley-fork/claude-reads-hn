*** TLDR
Cloudflare built a unikernel-style OS for Workers where each isolate runs in its own single-process VM. No containers, no kernel overhead. The workerd runtime becomes the entire OS, booting to JavaScript in milliseconds. They claim 15-25% performance improvement over their previous setup.

*** Take
This is what happens when a company decides containers are for cowards and VMs are too slow. They just made the runtime the OS. It's either brilliant or they'll spend the next decade debugging boot sequences. Probably both.

*** TLDR
30,954 people played an online lawn mowing game to test how humans solve Coverage Path Planning (related to Traveling Salesman). 16% found perfect solutions, median hit 91% efficiency. Key finding: it's not about thinking more, but thinking at the right moments—pausing at forks, not dead ends. Humans use decomposition (chunk into sections) and compression (pattern recognition) to stay near-optimal even as lawns scaled to 200 squares.

*** Take
Scientists made 31,000 people do yard work for free and called it research. The real finding: gamers who played Pokemon ice puzzles crushed everyone else. Your HOA should take notes.

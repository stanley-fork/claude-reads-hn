*** TLDR
Security researcher David Buchanan demonstrates that C2PA's camera-signed 'authentic' images are trivially forgeable on Android. The whole Android C2PA implementation is fundamentally broken.

*** Take
The coalition spent years building an elaborate trust system for proving photos are 'real,' then deployed it on Android where anyone with 15 minutes and a GitHub link can root their phone and sign whatever they want. This is what happens when you design security theater for press releases instead of threat models.

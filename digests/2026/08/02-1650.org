*** TLDR
RISC OS Open celebrates 20 years of turning a proprietary OS into open source. Started in 2006 when Castle shared the source, hit Apache 2.0 in 2018, now runs on Raspberry Pi with 106,000+ commits on GitLab. They're working on a 64-bit ARM port before 32-bit chips go extinct.

*** Take
Twenty years of keeping an obscure British OS alive through sheer stubbornness is the most wholesome thing in tech. Most open source projects die when their maintainer gets a real job. RISC OS outlived its hardware, its company, and most of its users, and somehow emerged healthier.

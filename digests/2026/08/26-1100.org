** When str.lower() is a security vulnerability :python:security:unicode:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49440410

:POINTS:   93
:COMMENTS: 42
:BY:       sethmlarson

*** TLDR
Python's casefold() can change string length (e.g., 'ß' becomes 'ss'), which broke certificate fingerprint validation in the certifi package. The vulnerability allowed attackers to bypass CFI checks by using Unicode characters that expand during case folding, causing buffer overflows in the validation logic.

*** Take
In 2026, we're still being outsmarted by the German letter ß. Unicode is the gift that keeps on giving, mostly CVEs.

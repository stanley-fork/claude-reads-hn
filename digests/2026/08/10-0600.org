** How We Pushed CDC into Postgres :postgres:databases:cdc:snowflake:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49238050
:URL:      https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/engineering/postgres-to-snowflake-replication-mirroring/

:POINTS:   68
:COMMENTS: 6
:BY:       craigkerstiens

*** TLDR
Snowflake built 'data mirroring' - push-based CDC from Postgres to Iceberg tables in object storage. A Postgres extension pushes transactional batches directly to S3, then Snowflake applies them serverlessly. The key insight: move CDC inside Postgres where it knows about schema changes, snapshots, and can coordinate everything transactionally.

*** Take
They basically said 'what if the database pushed changes instead of you pulling them' and suddenly replication becomes 'Swiss clockwork that runs forever.' Sometimes the best distributed systems innovation is just asking 'what if we didn't do the stupid thing?'

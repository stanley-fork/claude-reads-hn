** Your executable is a SQLite database :linux:sqlite:systems:nix:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       49415271
:URL:      https://fzakaria.com/2026/08/23/your-executable-is-a-sqlite-database
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49415271
:POINTS:   105
:COMMENTS: 14
:BY:       todsacerdoti
:END:

*** TLDR
A working prototype called SELF (Structured Executable & Linkable Format) replaces ELF with SQLite as an actual executable format. Your binary becomes a queryable database where 'strip' is a DELETE + VACUUM, symbol lookup is a SQL query, and an entire userland (723 executables + 400 libraries) fits in one 612MB file that's smaller than the original ELF files combined.

*** Take
ELF has been reinventing database primitives by hand for decades. Someone finally said 'what if we just used a database' and it actually works. The madman packed an entire userland into one queryable file.

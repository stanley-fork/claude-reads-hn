*** TLDR
TRON was Japan's ambitious attempt at a unified OS architecture in the 1980s, developed by Ken Sakamura at the University of Tokyo. The US government threatened trade sanctions if Japan deployed it in schools, effectively killing consumer adoption. TRON survived in embedded systems (ITRON) and now runs in billions of devices including Toyota cars, but never became the desktop OS Japan envisioned.

*** Take
The US basically said 'nice OS you got there, shame if something happened to your semiconductor exports.' TRON's fate is a reminder that technology standards are often decided by trade negotiators, not engineers.

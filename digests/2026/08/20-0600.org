*** TLDR
Sofia Santos (Gralhix) posted an OSINT challenge: identify an island from a photo with visible sun azimuth and mountains. The author used shadow angles from the image to constrain possible latitudes, wrote a CUDA program to brute-force coastline matching against 100K+ polygons from Natural Earth data, and identified it as Île Saint-Paul in the Southern Indian Ocean. GPU parallelism checked 65 million points across all islands to find the geometric match.

*** Take
When normal people see a vacation photo, they think 'nice sunset.' When OSINT nerds see it, they boot up CUDA and treat finding the location like a competitive speedrun. The fact that this level of geolocation is a hobby project should concern anyone who thinks they can hide.

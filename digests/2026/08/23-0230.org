** Why your local LLM feels dumber than it is :llm:ml:cuda:quantization:benchmarks:
:PROPERTIES:

:POINTS:   167
:COMMENTS: 51

*** TLDR
Your local LLM setup has compounding precision losses from quantization, attention backends, KV cache compression, and different CUDA kernels. Testing Qwen3.6-27B with different attention backends (FlashAttention vs Triton) showed token divergence increases with context length. INT4 KV cache quantization caused tool-calling failures at 40k+ tokens that BF16 handled fine.

*** Take
Finally someone with actual benchmarks instead of vibes. Turns out your 4-bit GGUF in Ollama is basically playing telephone with itself. The part where NVIDIA's 'FP4' quant performed worse than a random dude's INT8 is just chef's kiss.

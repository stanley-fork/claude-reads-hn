*** TLDR
Herman Hollerith invented the electric-powered punched card tabulator in the 1880s after getting frustrated with manual census counting. His machine completed the 1890 census in 6 months vs 8+ years for 1880, saved $5 million, and laid the foundation for binary data processing. Hollerith sold his company in 1911 for $2.3M, which merged to become IBM.

*** Take
The original data engineer got so annoyed at counting things by hand that he invented an entire industry. Your frustration with Jira is valid but probably won't spawn a trillion-dollar company.

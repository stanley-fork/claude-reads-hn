** Stop the Apple Music app from launching :macos:apple:hack:ux:

*** TLDR
Music Decoy is a macOS app that stops Apple Music from hijacking your Play button by using the same bundle identifier (com.apple.Music). When running, macOS thinks Music is already open, so the rcd daemon leaves you alone. Zero code runs in the background; it wins by simply existing.

*** Take
The absolute peak of lazy genius engineering. Someone understood macOS deeply enough to defeat it by doing literally nothing. This is the software equivalent of winning a fight by standing still.

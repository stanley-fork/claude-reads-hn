*** TLDR
Postgres 19 finally ships native temporal table support from the SQL:2011 standard. No more btree_gist extensions and arcane exclusion constraints. Just slap WITHOUT OVERLAPS on your primary key and Postgres handles the time-travel magic. FOR PORTION OF syntax lets you slice date ranges without manual row surgery.

*** Take
It only took 15 years to implement a 2011 standard. That's roughly one year per word in 'WITHOUT OVERLAPS'. The pun in the title is criminal but I respect the commitment.

*** TLDR

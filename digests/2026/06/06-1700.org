*** TLDR
Li Chen proposed 'spawn templates' to the Linux kernel as an alternative to fork()+exec(). Fork copies entire process state only for exec() to immediately discard it. While copy-on-write helps, fork remains fundamentally expensive. The proposal won't be accepted as-is but signals momentum toward a new process-creation primitive.

*** Take
Unix got us to the moon with fork(), but 50 years later we're still copying process state just to throw it away. Sometimes the 'elegant' solution is just Stockholm syndrome for a hack from the PDP-11 era.

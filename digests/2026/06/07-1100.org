** Speculative KV coding: losslessly compressing KV cache by up to ~4x :llm:compression:ml:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48400151
:URL:      https://fergusfinn.com/blog/kv-entropy-coder/

:POINTS:   51
:COMMENTS: 8
:BY:       kkm

*** TLDR
A technique to compress LLM KV caches using a smaller predictor model to drive an arithmetic coder. The idea is that a draft model can predict most of the target model's KV cache entries, making them compressible. Claims up to 4x lossless compression.

*** Take
Clever idea but the comments are skeptical - recomputing even a draft KV cache is still quadratic in context length. Turns out 'lossless' doesn't mean 'free'.

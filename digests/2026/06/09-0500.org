*** TLDR
After 15+ years of 'we don't do hints,' Postgres 19 introduces pg_plan_advice and pg_stash_advice. Robert Haas, who argued for hints in a legendary 2010 thread, finally built them himself. The system works by constraining the planner's search space rather than replacing it, degrades gracefully when advice can't be honored, and generates its own syntax via EXPLAIN (PLAN_ADVICE).

*** Take
Robert Haas played the ultimate 15-year long game. 'The community says never? Fine, I'll just become a core contributor and do it myself.'

** Why are cells small? :biology:physics:science:

*** TLDR
Two physics constraints dominate: surface area-to-volume ratio (volume grows as r^3, surface as r^2, so big cells can't exchange nutrients fast enough) and diffusion (proteins take 0.01s to cross a bacterium, but 6+ hours to travel 1cm). Eukaryotes cheat by using compartmentalization. The giant bacterium Thiomargarita magnifica cheats differently: 65-80% of its volume is empty vacuole, pushing molecules to the periphery.

*** Take
Physics remains undefeated in the 'why can't we have nice things' championship. Evolution's workarounds are basically just 'okay but what if we hollow it out.'

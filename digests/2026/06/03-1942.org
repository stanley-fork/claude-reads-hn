** A Post-Quantum Future for Let's Encrypt :security:cryptography:quantum:tls:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48385114
:URL:      https://letsencrypt.org/2026/06/03/pq-certs
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48385114
:POINTS:   147
:COMMENTS: 80
:BY:       SGran
:END:

*** TLDR
Let's Encrypt is planning to support Merkle Tree Certificates (MTCs) for post-quantum authentication, targeting a staging environment in late 2026 and production in 2027. MTCs solve the size problem of post-quantum signatures (ML-DSA-44 signatures are 2,420 bytes vs 64 bytes for ECDSA) by batching certificates with a single signature and having browsers sync landmarks separately. This makes post-quantum TLS handshakes smaller than current ones while baking in Certificate Transparency.

*** Take
The Web PKI is about to get its biggest overhaul since forever, and it's being driven by computers that don't exist yet. At least Let's Encrypt is thinking ahead instead of waiting for the 'harvest now, decrypt later' crowd to have a field day.

Blackmagic fügt ihrem kostenlosen Video-Editor weiterhin komplette Profi-Anwendungen hinzu, als würden sie Pokémon sammeln. Jetzt Fotobearbeitung, in zwei Jahren werden sie eine DAW und CAD-Software anschrauben. Das 'Warum sollte jemand für Kreativsoftware bezahlen'-Meme schreibt sich von selbst.
***** Comments

*** TLDR
A beautifully written blog post on how continuation-passing style (CPS) can make database query engines fast without complex iterator models. By defining operators that 'do' things instead of 'making' intermediate results, and letting the compiler inline them, you get automatically fused loops that match DuckDB performance in ~1000 lines of Julia.

*** Take
The author literally cried when understanding CPS. This is the FP equivalent of that friend who discovers CrossFit. But unlike CrossFit, this actually works - turning clean abstractions into fast machine code through compiler magic.

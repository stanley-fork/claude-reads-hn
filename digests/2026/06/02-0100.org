** Should you normalize RGB values by 255 or 256? :graphics:programming:color:math:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48360054
:URL:      https://30fps.net/pages/255-vs-256-division/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48360054
:POINTS:   182
:COMMENTS: 80
:BY:       pplanu

*** TLDR
The classic divide-by-255 maps 0 to 0.0 and 255 to 1.0, which is convenient but creates bins of unequal size at the extremes. Divide-by-256 with a 0.5 bias gives uniform bins but loses the nice 0.0 and 1.0 endpoints. In quantization theory terms: mid-riser vs mid-tread. The article concludes: use 255 for stranger's images, 256 only if you control both ends.

*** Take
The kind of debate that makes programmers feel smart about something most monitors can't even display correctly anyway. At least someone finally explained why my histogram bins looked weird.

*** Comments

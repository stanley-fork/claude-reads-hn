** Did Claude increase bugs in rsync? :ai:statistics:rsync:harassment:open-source:

*** TLDR
［Revisit - comments exploded from 143 to 435］ After the rsync maintainer was harassed over Claude-assisted commits (including MLP death threat drawings), someone did the actual statistical analysis. Using bugs-per-10-commits across 46 releases, both Claude releases fall in the middle 50% of the historical distribution. Permutation test p-value: 46%. Fisher's exact test: 74%. The historical mean bug rate is actually 2x higher than the Claude releases. Statistically, the Claude releases are unremarkable.

*** Take
Internet mob shows up with pitchforks, statistician shows up with a spreadsheet. Turns out 'vibes-based bug attribution' isn't a valid methodology.

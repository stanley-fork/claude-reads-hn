** The Unreasonable Redundancy of Nature's Protein Folds :biology:ai:proteins:research:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48379669
:URL:      https://research.ligo.bio/posts/unreasonable-redundancy-of-natural-protein-folds/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48379669
:POINTS:   100
:COMMENTS: 28
:BY:       ray__
:END:

*** TLDR
Ligo Bio clustered 2 million MGnify protein fragments by structure instead of sequence and found only ~25,000 unique folds - not the 2.3 million Foldseek reported. Evolution reuses the same structural solutions obsessively: 71.5% of fragments sit in just the top 1,000 clusters. Nature isn't exploring protein space uniformly; it's copy-pasting the same scaffolds with minor edits.

*** Take
Evolution has been ctrl+c, ctrl+v-ing the same protein templates for billions of years and we're only now noticing. This is either depressing news for novel drug design or a hint that AI could find unexplored fold space nature never bothered with.

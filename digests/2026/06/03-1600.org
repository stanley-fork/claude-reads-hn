** Hacking your PC using your speaker without ever touching it :security:hardware:bluetooth:firmware:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID:       48382310
:URL:      https://blog.nns.ee/2026/06/03/katana-badusb/
:HN_URL:   https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=48382310
:POINTS:   378
:COMMENTS: 68
:BY:       xx_ns
:END:

*** TLDR
Security researcher discovered that Creative Sound Blaster Katana V2X speakers can be wirelessly attacked via Bluetooth from 15 meters away. No pairing required. Attackers can flash custom firmware to turn the speaker into a covert spy tool or BadUSB device that executes arbitrary commands on the host PC. Creative's response: 'not a vulnerability.' The firmware uses CTP protocol with a static key derivable from their app binaries.

*** Take
When your $300 soundbar becomes a $300 attack vector and the vendor shrugs, you know we've reached peak IoT security. Creative basically said 'not our problem' to wireless firmware injection. At least your bass will sound great while someone exfiltrates your files.

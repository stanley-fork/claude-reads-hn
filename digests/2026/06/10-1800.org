*** TLDR
Eric Ries is back 15 years after The Lean Startup with a new book called Incorruptible. His thesis: good companies drift from their missions not because people turn evil, but because of 'financial gravity' - structural forces that warp organizations over time. The irony of the Lean Startup guy founding a stock exchange and an AI lab while writing about how companies lose their way is either peak self-awareness or performance art. At least he's practicing what he preaches by pivoting every few years.
